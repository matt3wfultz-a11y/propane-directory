Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Cylinder Refill, Exchange & Bulk Delivery

October 23, 2026

Searching for propane gas near El Paso? You have three distinct ways to buy it — refill your own cylinder at a local station, swap it at an exchange cage, or set up bulk residential delivery for whole-home use. Each option has a different price per gallon and a different best use. This guide covers all three, with current 2026 El Paso pricing for each.

The Three Ways to Buy Propane Gas Near El Paso

1. Cylinder Refill (Best Value)

Take your existing cylinder to a local propane dealer or refill station. An attendant weighs the tank empty, fills it to capacity, and charges by the gallon. A 20-lb grill cylinder holds about 4.7 gallons and will run you $16–$21 at El Paso area dealers — roughly $3.40–$4.50 per gallon.

This is the cheapest way to buy propane gas per gallon in El Paso. Local dealers have lower overhead than the national exchange programs and fill tanks to their full 20-lb capacity, not the 15-lb partial fills used by exchange cages.

Where to refill near El Paso:

  • Local propane dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas bulk dealers, independent suppliers)
  • U-Haul centers — several El Paso locations fill 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders
  • Tractor Supply — outdoor fill station at select locations

2. Tank Exchange (Most Convenient)

Exchange cages are outside virtually every Walmart, Home Depot, H-E-B, and gas station in El Paso. You hand in your empty tank, pay at the register, and pick up a pre-filled replacement — no attendant, no appointment, available any time of day.

The cost: $24–$28 per swap through Blue Rhino or AmeriGas in El Paso. But the exchange tank only contains 15 lbs of propane (3.5 gallons), not the full 20-lb fill. That puts the effective price at $4.80–$5.50 per gallon — 30–40% more than a local refill.

3. Bulk Residential Delivery (For Whole-Home Use)

If you use propane for home heating, a whole-home generator, or a gas range and water heater combination, a fixed tank with regular delivery is the right solution. Propane companies like Ferrellgas and local independent dealers serve the El Paso metro with above-ground 120-gallon and 250-gallon tank installations.

Bulk delivery pricing is negotiated with the supplier and typically runs lower per gallon than cylinder refills — especially on annual service contracts. Call a local dealer directly for a quote based on your usage and location.

Which Option Is Right for You?

A quick decision guide:

  • Grill, patio heater, or fire pit: Cylinder refill — fill to full capacity, pay the lowest price per gallon. If the tank is old, exchange will accept it without a recertification.
  • Emergency or convenience fill: Exchange cage — available 24/7 at retail stores, no driving to a dealer, no wait for an attendant.
  • Home heating or whole-home gas: Bulk delivery with a fixed tank — call Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, or a local independent dealer for a quote.
  • Camping or small appliances: 1-lb disposable cylinders at hardware or sporting goods stores — expensive per gallon but the right format for single-burner stoves and lanterns.

Propane Gas in the Wider El Paso Metro

Propane gas is available throughout the metro area, not just central El Paso. Refill and exchange service extends into Horizon City, Socorro, Canutillo, and Fabens along the Rio Grande valley. Just across the New Mexico line, Las Cruces has its own cluster of dealers serving the southern NM side of the metro.
