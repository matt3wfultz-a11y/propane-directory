If you're searching for propane gas near El Paso, TX, the city has a solid network of local suppliers — from neighborhood hardware stores with exchange cages to dedicated propane dealers who refill cylinders of any size and deliver bulk gas to residential and commercial properties. Types of Propane Gas Service Available Near El Paso

Propane gas service in El Paso falls into three main categories. Understanding which one applies to you will help you find the right supplier and avoid overpaying.

  • Cylinder refill: You bring your empty tank (any size from 20 lb to 100 lb) to a local propane dealer or refill station, and they fill it by weight. This is the most cost-effective option for grill, patio heater, and camp stove users.
  • Tank exchange: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas operate exchange cages at retail locations across El Paso. You swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at the register. Convenient, but more expensive per gallon than refill.
  • Bulk delivery: For residential water heaters, whole-home heating, or commercial use, a propane company delivers gas directly to a large tank (120-gallon to 1,000-gallon) on your property. Ferrellgas and other regional carriers serve the El Paso metro area with scheduled and will-call delivery.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

Propane gas suppliers are distributed across the El Paso metro — from the Northeast side near Loop 375 to the Lower Valley along I-10 and the Westside near UTEP. Key places to look:

  • Dedicated propane dealers: Local and national dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas bulk accounts, independent operators) offer the lowest per-gallon prices and fill tanks of any size. Many also sell and service larger residential tanks.
  • U-Haul locations: Multiple U-Haul centers in El Paso offer walk-in cylinder refill at competitive prices. No appointment required for standard 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders.
  • Tractor Supply: Select El Paso-area Tractor Supply locations have outdoor propane refill stations — a good option for residents in the East El Paso and Horizon City area.
  • Retail exchange cages: Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and most major grocery chains in El Paso carry Blue Rhino or AmeriGas exchange cages for quick tank swaps.

Common Uses for Propane Gas in El Paso

El Paso's climate and housing mix create year-round demand for propane across several applications:

  • Outdoor grilling: The most common use for 20-lb cylinders. El Paso's mild winters mean grilling season runs nearly all year.
  • Patio and outdoor heaters: During cooler desert nights from October through March, patio heaters see heavy use at homes and local restaurants.
  • Residential heating and water heating: Homes in outlying areas — Horizon City, Canutillo, Fabens — that lack natural gas service often use propane for heating and water heating.
  • Construction and trades: Propane powers roofing torches, temporary heaters on job sites, and forklifts at commercial facilities throughout the El Paso industrial corridor.
  • RV travel: I-10 through El Paso is a major RV corridor connecting the Southwest. Local propane dealers along the highway serve travelers passing through.
