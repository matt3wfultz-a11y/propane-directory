Propane Gas for Your Home: Setting Up Delivery Service

If you need propane gas for home heating, a backup generator, or a similar fixed installation, setting up service generally looks like this:

  • Get quotes from a few suppliers. Per-gallon rates and contract terms vary, so it's worth calling more than one El Paso-area dealer.
  • Decide on a tank. Residential setups typically use a 120-gallon (vertical, common for small homes), 250-gallon, or 500-gallon tank. Some suppliers lease the tank as part of your service agreement; others let you purchase one outright for more flexibility on future fills.
  • Schedule installation. The supplier delivers and sets the tank, runs the gas line to your appliances, and performs a leak check before turning on service.
  • Choose a delivery plan. Automatic delivery schedules fills based on your estimated usage and the season; will-call means you contact the supplier when you're running low. Automatic delivery avoids the risk of running out mid-winter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get propane gas delivered to my home in El Paso?

Contact a supplier that offers residential bulk delivery — a national provider like AmeriGas or Ferrellgas, or a local El Paso dealer. They'll install or fill a tank on your property (typically 120, 250, or 500 gallons) and set you up on a delivery schedule, either automatic or will-call.

How much does propane gas cost in El Paso?

Bulk residential delivery typically runs $2.80–$3.80 per gallon. A 20-lb cylinder refill costs $16–$21 (about $3.40–$4.50/gal effective), while a retail exchange swap costs $24–$28 for only 15 lbs ($4.80–$5.50/gal effective).

Do I need to buy my own propane tank for home delivery?

Not always — many suppliers lease or install a tank as part of a delivery agreement, sometimes with a requirement to buy exclusively from that company. Owning your tank outright gives you the freedom to shop around for the best per-gallon rate.

Where can I get propane gas for my grill or RV near El Paso?

Local propane dealers, U-Haul locations, and select Tractor Supply stores refill 20-lb, 30-lb, and 33-lb cylinders. Retail exchange cages at Walmart, Home Depot, and most grocery stores offer 24/7 swaps but fill to only 15 lbs. See our El Paso propane directory for verified locations near you.
