If you need propane gas near El Paso, TX, you have more options than the exchange cage at your nearest grocery store. 2. Refill Stations at Retail Locations (Convenient, Mid-Price)

Several retail chains operate propane refill stations across El Paso:

  • U-Haul — Multiple El Paso locations fill propane by weight. Call ahead to confirm the station is staffed.
  • Tractor Supply — Outdoor fill station available at select El Paso area stores.

3. Exchange Cages (Most Convenient, Highest Per-Gallon Cost)

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are the most visible source of propane gas in El Paso — you'll find them outside nearly every Walmart Supercenter, Home Depot, and H-E-B across the city. Exchange is fast and requires no appointment, but the gas costs more per gallon and the tanks are only filled to 15 lbs — not the full 20-lb capacity.

Propane Gas for Specific Uses in El Paso

Grilling and Patio

The standard 20-lb grill cylinder is the most common tank size in El Paso. Local dealers and U-Haul stations fill it for $16–$21. Exchange cages are convenient but cost more. If you grill regularly, switching to a local refill station typically saves $40–$80 per season.

Camping and RV

Camping near El Paso — at Hueco Tanks State Park, Franklin Mountains State Park, or along the I-10 corridor — requires propane for cooking and heating. U-Haul and local dealers near the freeway are your best options for a refill before heading out. See our full guide: Propane for Camping and RVs.

Residential Heating and Appliances

Homes in West Texas and the El Paso area that use propane for heating, water heaters, or stoves typically contract with a local bulk supplier for regular delivery. West Texas Propane, Ferrellgas, and AmeriGas bulk all serve the El Paso residential market. Call for delivery quotes — rates vary by tank size and contract terms.

Construction and Commercial

Contractors and commercial users needing propane for forklifts, temporary heating, or industrial equipment can work with local propane dealers directly. Most El Paso dealers carry 33-lb and larger cylinders and can arrange delivery.

El Paso vs. National Propane Prices

The US Energy Information Administration (EIA) tracks residential propane prices weekly. El Paso consistently sits at or below the national average, reflecting its position at the crossroads of Texas and New Mexico supply networks. In 2026, the national residential average has ranged from $2.40–$2.90 per gallon for bulk residential delivery, while retail refill prices in El Paso run $3.40–$4.50 per gallon — higher than bulk but still competitive for small-cylinder service.
