Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Where to Buy & Current Prices

August 28, 2026

Searching for propane gas near El Paso? Whether you need a quick tank refill, a new filled cylinder, or bulk delivery to your home, El Paso has multiple local options across the city. This guide covers every way to buy propane gas in the El Paso area, what you'll pay per gallon in 2026, and how to get the best deal.

Ways to Buy Propane Gas Near El Paso

There are four main ways residents and businesses in El Paso get propane gas, each suited to different needs:

  • Cylinder refill: Bring your empty tank to a refill station and have it filled by weight. The cheapest per-gallon option. Available at dedicated propane dealers, U-Haul locations, and select hardware stores across El Paso.
  • Tank exchange: Drop off your empty cylinder and pick up a pre-filled one at an exchange cage. Convenient but more expensive — exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity. Found outside Walmart, Home Depot, and most grocery stores in El Paso.
  • Delivered cylinder: Some El Paso propane dealers deliver filled cylinders directly to your address. Useful for 100-lb tanks and larger that are difficult to transport.
  • Bulk residential delivery: If you have a 250-gallon or larger tank at your home, a local propane company delivers directly to your tank. Prices are negotiated per gallon and vary by supplier and delivery volume.

Where to Buy Propane Gas Near El Paso

El Paso has propane gas available across multiple neighborhoods. Here's where to look by supplier type:

  • Dedicated propane dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas bulk, independent suppliers): Offer the lowest per-gallon prices and fill tanks of all sizes. Located across the city and along major corridors including Montana Avenue, Dyer Street, and the Lower Valley.
  • U-Haul centers: Several El Paso U-Haul locations offer propane refill by weight, typically at prices close to local dealers. A reliable option for 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders.
  • Tractor Supply: El Paso-area locations offer outdoor propane refill service. Prices are competitive and hours are convenient.
  • Exchange cages (Blue Rhino / AmeriGas): Found at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, HEB, and most grocery stores in El Paso. Convenient if you need gas immediately and don't want to transport an empty tank across town — but you'll pay more per gallon.

Refill vs. Exchange: Which Is the Better Deal in El Paso?

If you have the time to visit a refill station, the math strongly favors a refill over an exchange every time:

OptionGas You ReceiveTypical El Paso PriceCost Per Gallon
Refill (local dealer)~20 lbs / 4.7 gal full fill$16–$21$3.40–$4.50
Exchange (cage)~15 lbs / 3.5 gal only$24–$28$4.80–$5.50

An exchange saves you a trip but costs $7–$12 more per tank and delivers 25% less gas. Over a full summer grilling season, that gap adds up quickly. Exchange only when speed matters more than cost — like when you run out mid-cookout on a Sunday evening.
