Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Find Local Suppliers & Current Prices

Whether you need propane gas for your backyard grill, patio heater, camping trip, or home appliances, El Paso has strong local supply. How El Paso Gets Its Propane

El Paso sits along major energy corridors connecting Texas, New Mexico, and Mexico, giving it reliable access to propane supply year-round. The city is served by national propane distributors (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, Suburban Propane) alongside independent local dealers. This competition keeps El Paso propane prices at or below the Texas average.

Retail exchange cages (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas) are available at virtually every big-box store and many gas stations across the city. Dedicated refill stations — where you pay by the gallon and get a complete fill — are spread across the Westside, Northeast, and Lower Valley.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

1. Local Propane Dealers (Best Price)

Independent and national-chain propane dealers offer the lowest per-gallon price in El Paso. They fill cylinders of all sizes — from 20-lb grill tanks to 100-lb residential cylinders — and most also offer bulk delivery for fixed residential tanks.

Dealers to look for in El Paso: Ferrellgas, AmeriGas supply points, Superior Propane, and independent local operators. Use our El Paso directory to find addresses and phone numbers for verified locations near you.

2. U-Haul Locations (Reliable Mid-Tier)

Multiple U-Haul centers in El Paso fill 20-lb and 33-lb propane cylinders. They fill by weight and post consistent pricing — a good middle-ground option when a dedicated dealer isn't convenient.

3. Farm and Ranch Supply Stores

Tractor Supply locations near El Paso operate outdoor propane fill stations during store hours. Pricing is competitive and staff are trained on safety handling.

4. Retail Exchange Cages (Most Convenient, Highest Cost)

Exchange cages are available at:

  • Walmart (multiple El Paso locations)
  • Home Depot and Lowe's
  • Kroger and H-E-B grocery stores
  • Many Circle K, Stripes, and Murphy USA gas stations

Exchange is fast and requires no appointment or waiting — but you pay a significant premium per gallon and receive only a partial fill (see pricing below).

Propane Gas for Specific Uses Near El Paso

Grilling and Outdoor Cooking

El Paso's outdoor lifestyle and year-round mild weather make propane grills a staple. A standard 20-lb cylinder ($16–$21 to refill) provides 18–20 hours of grilling at medium heat. Refill at any of the dealer or U-Haul locations listed in our directory. Avoid exchange cages unless it's a genuine emergency — you'll get less gas for more money.

Patio and Garage Heaters

Even in El Paso, winter evenings dip into the 30s and 40s. Outdoor patio heaters typically burn through a 20-lb cylinder in 8–10 hours at high setting. Keep a spare tank on hand — refilling both at once at a dealer saves a second trip and a second fill fee.

Camping and Tailgating

For camping stoves and lanterns, 1-lb camping cylinders are sold at sporting goods stores and Walmart for $3–$7 each. For extended trips or larger camp stoves, a refillable 5-lb or 11-lb cylinder is more economical. U-Haul and dealer stations can fill these sizes.

Home Appliances and Residential Use

Some El Paso homes — particularly in rural areas, new developments, and properties west of the city — rely on propane for water heating, cooking, or whole-home heating. Residential service uses fixed tanks (100–500 gallons) filled by bulk delivery. Contact a local propane dealer for a site assessment and delivery quote. El Paso dealers typically offer auto-fill programs that monitor your tank level and schedule deliveries before you run out.

Propane Gas Safety Tips for El Paso

  • Store tanks outdoors: Never store propane cylinders inside a home, garage, or vehicle. Keep them upright in a well-ventilated outdoor area away from heat sources.
  • Check expiration dates: Cylinders must be recertified 12 years after manufacture and every 5 years after that. The date is stamped on the collar. An expired tank will be refused at a refill station — take it to an exchange cage if you need a replacement.
  • Transport upright: Keep cylinders standing upright in your vehicle. In an enclosed car or SUV, crack windows and transport them directly to your destination without extended stops.
  • Smell for leaks: Propane has a strong rotten-egg odor added deliberately. If you smell it near a connection or appliance, turn off the valve, move away, and call your dealer or 911.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find propane gas near El Paso?

El Paso has propane at dedicated dealer refill stations, U-Haul locations, Tractor Supply, and retail exchange cages at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and most major grocery stores and gas stations. For the best price, use a dealer refill station. See our El Paso directory for addresses and phone numbers.

What does propane cost per gallon in El Paso?

At local dealer refill stations, propane gas costs $3.40–$4.50 per gallon in El Paso in 2026. Exchange cages run $4.80–$5.50 per gallon effective cost (and only fill your tank to 15 lbs rather than the full 20-lb rated capacity). Bulk residential delivery is typically $2.75–$3.75 per gallon for large fixed tanks.

How do I get propane gas delivered to my home in El Paso?

Contact a local propane dealer about residential delivery. You'll need a fixed tank installed on your property — dealers can lease or sell you one. Once set up, many offer automatic delivery programs that monitor your level and schedule refills before you run low. This is the most cost-effective way to use propane gas for home appliances.

What's the difference between propane refill and propane exchange?

At a refill station, you bring your cylinder and a dealer fills it to full capacity (20 lbs / ~4.7 gallons) and charges you by the gallon ($3.40–$4.50/gal). At an exchange cage, you hand in your cylinder and receive a different one pre-filled to only 15 lbs — and pay a flat fee ($24–$28) that works out to $4.80–$5.50/gal. Refill is almost always cheaper unless your tank is expired or damaged. See our full refill vs exchange comparison.
