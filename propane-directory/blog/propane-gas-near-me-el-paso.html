Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Suppliers, Prices & Stations

If you're searching for propane gas near El Paso, TX, you have solid options across the city — from local dealers who fill tanks the same day to exchange cages open around the clock and bulk delivery services for home heating. El Paso's location along the Texas-New Mexico supply corridor keeps propane gas prices competitive compared to much of the country. Here's how to find gas near you and what to expect to pay.

How to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

The fastest way to locate propane gas near you is our El Paso propane directory — a verified list of local suppliers with addresses, phone numbers, and one-click directions. But here's a quick breakdown of the main source types in the area:

  • Local propane dealers: Companies like Ferrellgas, Superior Plus Propane, and regional independents supply propane gas for cylinder refills, bulk delivery, and residential tank installation. These offer the widest selection and lowest per-gallon rates.
  • U-Haul locations: Several El Paso U-Haul centers stock propane gas for cylinder refills. They fill by weight, are open extended hours, and serve 20-lb, 33-lb, and 40-lb tanks.
  • Exchange cages (Blue Rhino / AmeriGas): Pre-filled 20-lb cylinders are available at most Walmart, Home Depot, Walgreens, and grocery store locations across El Paso. Convenient but more expensive per gallon.
  • Hardware and farm stores: Select Tractor Supply locations in the El Paso area offer propane gas refill service, typically at prices below the exchange cages.

Propane Gas for Home Heating Near El Paso

While El Paso's mild winters mean most residents don't rely on propane gas for primary heating, many homes in the surrounding rural areas — particularly in the Lower Valley, East El Paso County, and communities along the New Mexico border — use propane for furnaces, water heaters, and ranges.

For bulk propane gas delivery in the El Paso area, established dealers typically offer:

  • Automatic fill programs (tank refilled on a schedule before it runs low)
  • Will-call service (you call when you need a delivery)
  • Emergency delivery for out-of-gas situations
  • Tank rental or purchase with installation

Bulk pricing runs $2.80–$3.80 per gallon in the El Paso area, depending on volume and contract terms. Larger orders typically unlock lower per-gallon rates.
