Where to Get Propane Gas Near El Paso

Propane gas is available through three main channels in El Paso:

  • Local propane dealers and refill stations: Dedicated suppliers like DAE Propane, West Texas Propane, Bob's Propane, and AmeriGas bulk dealers fill cylinders of all sizes on-site. These are your best source for the lowest per-gallon price and the widest range of tank sizes (20 lb through 100 lb and beyond).
  • Retail exchange cages: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most major gas stations across El Paso. Convenient for standard 20-lb grill cylinders — but you pay more per gallon and only get 15 lbs of gas, not a full 20.
  • Bulk propane delivery: For home heating, large appliances, or agriculture, a dealer delivers propane directly to a permanent tank on your property. Bulk delivery runs the lowest per-gallon rate of all — typically $2.80–$3.80/gal in the El Paso area — but requires a minimum order and tank installation.

Propane Gas for Specific Uses Near El Paso

Depending on what you need the propane for, the right source changes:

  • Grilling and outdoor cooking: A 20-lb cylinder refill at a local dealer is your best value. Refill dealers across the Eastside, Westside, and Northeast El Paso can top off a standard grill tank in minutes.
  • Patio heaters and fire pits: Same as grilling — refill a 20-lb or 40-lb cylinder at a dealer for the lowest per-gallon rate.
  • RV and camping: Look for dealers that handle 33-lb forklift cylinders or have DOT-approved RV refill connections. U-Haul locations in El Paso also fill standard cylinders by weight. See our guide on propane for camping and RVs for more detail.
  • Home heating and appliances: Contact a bulk propane dealer — Ferrellgas, Superior Plus Propane, or a local independent — for a residential tank installation and automatic delivery schedule.
  • Commercial and agricultural: Larger-volume users should contact propane dealers directly to discuss contract pricing, which is typically lower than retail rack rates.
