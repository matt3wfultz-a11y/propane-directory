Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Suppliers, Prices & Services

Searching for propane gas near El Paso, TX? Whether you need a quick cylinder refill for your grill, bulk delivery to a residential tank, or a supplier for your business, El Paso has well-established propane gas service throughout the city and surrounding communities. This guide covers the types of propane gas service available, what you'll pay in 2026, and how to find a supplier near your zip code.

Types of Propane Gas Service Near El Paso

El Paso propane gas suppliers offer several service types. Understanding which one fits your situation determines where to look and what you'll pay:

Cylinder Refill (20 lb – 100 lb)

If you use propane for a backyard grill, patio heater, fire pit, or portable generator, a cylinder refill is what you need. Local dealers and select Tractor Supply and U-Haul locations fill portable cylinders by weight while you wait. This is the most affordable way to buy propane gas in El Paso — typically $3.40–$4.50 per gallon for a standard fill.

Tank Exchange (Convenience Stores & Retail)

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages outside Walmart, Home Depot, and major El Paso grocery stores let you swap an empty cylinder for a pre-filled one in minutes. Exchange is the most convenient option but costs more per gallon and delivers only 15 lbs instead of a full 20-lb fill. See our propane exchange guide for El Paso for cage locations.

Bulk Delivery (250-gallon to 1,000-gallon tanks)

Homeowners and businesses with a large propane tank on-site get scheduled or on-call bulk delivery from local propane gas companies. In El Paso, this covers whole-home heating, water heating, cooking ranges, backup generators, and commercial applications. Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, and regional independent dealers serve the greater El Paso metro area with delivery trucks.

Commercial & Agricultural Propane

El Paso's agricultural areas to the east and commercial corridor along I-10 are served by dealers offering metered service, large-volume bulk delivery, and on-site tank lease programs for construction, warehousing, and farming operations.

What Propane Gas Is Used for in El Paso

Propane gas serves a wide range of uses across the El Paso metro area:

  • Outdoor grilling and cooking: The most common use — standard 20-lb cylinders power millions of gas grills and flat-top griddles.
  • Patio and space heaters: El Paso winters can drop below freezing overnight. Propane patio heaters extend outdoor living into the cold months, and indoor propane space heaters are common in older homes.
  • RV and camping: Travelers along I-10 and I-25, Fort Bliss personnel, and campers heading to Hueco Tanks, Franklin Mountains, or the Guadalupe Mountains rely on propane for cooking, heating, and refrigeration. See our propane for camping and RV guide.
  • Whole-home heating and appliances: Propane powers furnaces, water heaters, ranges, and dryers in El Paso homes that aren't connected to natural gas lines — especially in outlying areas like Canutillo, Fabens, and Horizon City.
  • Backup generators: Many El Paso homeowners use propane-fueled generators to ride out the occasional severe weather outage.
  • Commercial and agricultural use: Greenhouses, crop drying, forklifts, and construction heating in the Rio Grande Valley east of El Paso are all common propane applications.

Propane Gas vs. Natural Gas in El Paso

Much of El Paso has access to natural gas distribution through El Paso Electric and local utility pipelines. Where natural gas is available, it typically runs cheaper per BTU than propane for high-volume residential uses like whole-home heating. However, propane wins in areas without gas line access, for portable applications, for backup power, and for equipment that doesn't justify a gas line installation. How to Find the Best Propane Gas Supplier Near You in El Paso

A few tips for choosing the right propane gas provider in El Paso:

  • For cylinder refills: Call ahead to confirm the dealer is open, get the current price per gallon, and ask if they fill the size cylinder you have. Hours vary significantly between dealers and chain locations.
  • For bulk delivery: Get at least two quotes. Ask about the minimum delivery amount, delivery fees, tank rental versus ownership, and whether they lock in a price for the season.
  • For exchange: Any big-box store or grocery store with an outdoor exchange cage works — the main brands (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas) charge similar prices across El Paso locations.
