Types of Propane Gas Service in El Paso

Propane gas is sold and delivered several different ways in El Paso. Knowing which type you need will save you time and money:

  • Cylinder refill: You bring your own cylinder (20 lb, 33 lb, 40 lb, or larger) to a local dealer or fill station, and they fill it by weight. This is the cheapest way to buy propane gas for grills, heaters, and portable appliances.
  • Tank exchange: Drop off an empty cylinder and pick up a pre-filled one at an exchange cage (Blue Rhino or AmeriGas) outside a retailer. Convenient but more expensive per gallon, and exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs rather than the rated 20.
  • Bulk residential delivery: A propane truck delivers directly to a large tank (120–1,000 gallons) at your home or business. Used for whole-home heating, water heaters, cooking ranges, and generators. Priced per gallon on delivery, often with a seasonal or automatic-fill contract.
  • Portable cylinder sales: New cylinders — from 1-lb disposable camping canisters to 100-lb residential cylinders — sold at hardware stores, Walmart, and propane dealers.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near You in El Paso

Cylinder Refill Stations

The most affordable way to get propane gas in El Paso is a direct refill at a local dealer or fill station. Locations include:

  • Independent propane dealers across El Paso's Westside, Northeast, and Lower Valley corridors
  • U-Haul centers (fill 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders by weight)
  • Tractor Supply Co. outdoor fill stations
  • Select hardware stores with outdoor propane fill service

Exchange Cage Locations

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are located at most major retailers in El Paso, including Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and Kroger. Exchange is convenient for a last-minute fill before a cookout, but costs significantly more per gallon than a refill.

Bulk Residential & Commercial Delivery

If you use propane gas to heat your home, run appliances, or power a generator, you'll need a bulk supplier with a delivery account. Major providers serving El Paso include Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, and several local independent dealers. Delivery is typically priced per gallon, with minimums and seasonal contracts available.

Propane Gas for Specific Uses in El Paso

  • Gas grills: A standard 20-lb cylinder is the right choice. Refill at a local dealer for the best price.
  • Patio and outdoor heaters: Most use a standard 20-lb cylinder. Check the BTU rating to estimate burn time — a 40,000 BTU heater runs roughly 10 hours on a full 20-lb tank.
  • RVs and campers: Most RVs run two 20-lb or 30-lb cylinders. Refill stations at U-Haul, Tractor Supply, and propane dealers handle these. See our RV and camping propane guide for details on filling larger tanks on the road.
  • Home heating and appliances: You'll need a bulk delivery account with a licensed propane company. They install or rent the tank and deliver on a schedule or when you call.
  • Generators: Portable propane generators typically use a 20-lb or 40-lb cylinder. Standby generators are usually piped to a large buried tank and require bulk delivery.
