Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Suppliers, Prices & Locations

October 30, 2026

If you're looking for propane gas near El Paso, TX, you have a good range of local suppliers to choose from. El Paso is served by dedicated propane dealers, national brands like Ferrellgas and AmeriGas, and dozens of retail refill and exchange points spread across the metro area. Whether you need a grill tank filled, a large residential cylinder, or bulk delivery to a farm or business, this guide points you to the right source.

Types of Propane Gas Service in El Paso

Not every "propane gas near me" search is the same. Here are the main service types available in El Paso and what each is best for:

Cylinder Refill (Best Value)

Take your empty cylinder to a local dealer or refill station. A technician weighs it, fills it to capacity, and you're done in minutes. Best for 20-lb, 33-lb, 40-lb, and 100-lb cylinders. El Paso refill prices run $3.40–$4.50 per gallon — below the national average, thanks to the city's location near major supply infrastructure along the Texas–New Mexico corridor.

Tank Exchange (Most Convenient)

Swap your empty tank for a pre-filled one at a Blue Rhino or AmeriGas cage outside Walmart, H-E-B, Home Depot, or Lowe's. Fast and available 24/7. Drawback: exchange tanks are filled to only 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity — so you pay more per gallon and get less gas. Reserve exchanges for when time matters most.

Bulk / Residential Delivery

For homes or businesses running large propane appliances — whole-home heat, hot water heaters, generators, or commercial cooking — a bulk dealer delivers directly to a 250-, 500-, or 1,000-gallon tank at your property. El Paso has several bulk propane suppliers; pricing is negotiated by the load and is substantially lower per gallon than retail cylinder fills.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

  • Local propane dealers: Ferrellgas, Suburban Propane, and independent dealers serve El Paso with both cylinder fill and bulk delivery. These offer the widest service range and lowest prices.
  • U-Haul locations: Multiple El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill for standard 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders, open during store hours.
  • Tractor Supply: Select El Paso-area Tractor Supply stores have outdoor propane fill stations for 20-lb cylinders.
  • Exchange cages: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas cages are available at most major retailers — Walmart, Home Depot, H-E-B, Lowe's — throughout the city.

El Paso's Propane Advantage

El Paso sits along major pipeline and distribution corridors connecting Texas to New Mexico and beyond, which keeps local propane supply consistent and prices generally competitive with the rest of Texas. The warm climate means lower residential heating demand compared to northern states, so supply rarely tightens the way it can in colder regions during winter.

Propane Gas for Specific Needs in El Paso

  • Grilling & outdoor cooking: A 20-lb refill is the lowest-cost option. At local El Paso dealers, one fill typically costs $16–$21 and lasts 18–25 hours on a standard grill.
  • Patio heaters: A 20-lb tank runs most patio heaters for 8–12 hours on high. Refill before a gathering, or keep a spare on hand.
  • Camping & RV: 1-lb disposable canisters are available at sporting goods stores. For larger RV tanks, U-Haul and dedicated dealers can fill motorhome and trailer cylinders. See our camping and RV guide for more detail.
  • Home heating & appliances: A local bulk dealer is the right call. They'll assess your usage, recommend tank size, and set up a delivery schedule.
