Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Dealers, Prices & Where to Buy

November 13, 2026

Searching for propane gas near El Paso, TX? The city has a solid network of propane suppliers — from independent dealers and national chains to exchange cages at every major retailer. Whether you need a 20-lb grill cylinder refilled, a 100-lb tank for home use, or bulk delivery for a business, this guide covers your options and what you'll pay in 2026.

Types of Propane Gas Services Near El Paso

Propane gas in the El Paso area is available through several different channels, depending on how much you need and how you plan to use it:

  • Cylinder refill: Bring your empty tank to a local dealer or refill station and pay by the gallon. The best-value option for regular propane users.
  • Cylinder exchange: Swap your empty cylinder at a retail cage (Blue Rhino or AmeriGas) for a pre-filled replacement. Convenient, but costs more per gallon and delivers less gas.
  • Bulk delivery: For homes or businesses with large above-ground or underground tanks (120 gallons and up), a propane company delivers directly to your property. Ferrellgas, Suburban Propane, and regional dealers serve the El Paso area.
  • New cylinder purchase: Buy an empty cylinder at a hardware store and get it filled at a local refill station.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

Local Propane Dealers

El Paso's dedicated propane dealers offer the widest range of services — refill, bulk delivery, tank sales, and service for residential and commercial customers. National chains with a local presence include Ferrellgas and AmeriGas (bulk operations), and independent regional suppliers also serve the area. Dealers typically offer the lowest per-gallon price and can fill any cylinder size from 20 lbs up to large commercial tanks.

Retail Refill Stations

Several retail locations across El Paso operate outdoor propane refill stations where an attendant fills your cylinder while you wait:

  • U-Haul locations — multiple El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill for 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders, priced by weight
  • Tractor Supply — outdoor refill station at El Paso-area stores; competitive pricing
  • Some Ace Hardware / True Value stores — select locations offer refill alongside exchange cages

Exchange Cages (Convenience Option)

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are available at virtually every Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most gas stations across El Paso. Exchange is fast and doesn't require an attendant, but the trade-off is cost: exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs (not the full 20-lb capacity), and the per-gallon price is 30–50% higher than a local refill.

Refill vs Exchange: Which Is Better for El Paso Propane Users?

For most El Paso residents, refilling is the smarter choice:

  • A 20-lb refill costs $16–$21 and delivers a full 4.7 gallons
  • An exchange costs $24–$28 and delivers only 3.5 gallons (15 lbs)
  • That's a $7–$12 difference per fill — plus you get 25% more gas with a refill

Exchange makes sense in a few specific situations: when your tank is past its certification date and needs swapping out, when it's late and no refill stations are open, or when you're in a pinch mid-cookout and the nearest option is a Walmart.

Propane for Home Use Near El Paso

If you use propane to heat your home, run a water heater, or fuel a whole-home generator, you're looking at bulk propane delivery rather than cylinder service. El Paso's climate — with cold winters and high elevation on the west side — makes propane an important fuel for many homes, particularly those not connected to natural gas infrastructure.

Bulk propane dealers serving the El Paso area include national suppliers like Ferrellgas and Suburban Propane, as well as regional independents. Most offer automatic delivery programs tied to weather-based usage estimates, and prices are typically lower per gallon than cylinder refill rates when you're buying in volume.
