Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Cylinders, Delivery & Prices

October 16, 2026

If you're looking for propane gas near El Paso, TX, the right source depends on what you're fueling. A grill or patio heater takes a portable 20-lb cylinder; a whole-home heating system or generator needs bulk residential delivery. El Paso is well-served for both — this guide covers every way to get propane gas locally, with current prices for each option.

Propane Gas for Grills and Portable Equipment

The most common need is a filled 20-lb cylinder for a gas grill, fire pit, or portable patio heater. In El Paso, you have two ways to get one:

Refill a Cylinder at a Local Station

A refill fills your own cylinder to its full rated capacity — typically about 4.7 gallons (20 lbs) of propane gas. It's the most cost-effective option, running $16–$21 per 20-lb fill at El Paso refill stations. Sources include:

  • U-Haul locations — multiple El Paso stores offer propane refill by weight; no appointment needed
  • Tractor Supply Co. — outdoor fill station; open during store hours
  • Local propane dealers (Ferrellgas, independent suppliers) — lowest per-gallon rate, handles all cylinder sizes
  • Some hardware stores — Ace Hardware and True Value locations with outdoor fill equipment

Full guide: Propane Refill Near Me in El Paso, TX.

Exchange at a Retail Cage

Exchange lets you swap an empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at a cage outside the store. It's available 24/7 at virtually every major El Paso retailer — Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most gas stations. Cost is $24–$28 per exchange.

The catch: exchange tanks are filled to only 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity. You pay more and get less gas — roughly 25% less propane for 25–50% more cost per gallon.

Propane Gas for Home Heating and Appliances

If you heat your home with propane, run a propane range or water heater, or power a standby generator, you need a residential bulk delivery service rather than portable cylinders. El Paso propane gas delivery works like this:

  • A supplier installs or rents a large propane tank on your property (typically 120–500 gallons)
  • You schedule delivery — either on-demand when your tank runs low, or on automatic refill programs where the supplier monitors usage
  • Price is set per gallon; residential rates in the El Paso area typically run $2.50–$3.50/gallon for bulk delivery, lower per unit than portable cylinders

Major residential propane gas suppliers serving the El Paso metro area include Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, and several local independent dealers. Call for a quote — prices vary by contract type and current market rates.

Propane Gas for RVs and Camping

Traveling through El Paso with an RV or heading out to Hueco Tanks, Franklin Mountains, or the Sacramento Mountains? Propane gas for RV travel uses the same refill infrastructure as residential cylinders. El Paso is a strong stopping point:

  • Several RV parks along I-10 and I-25 near El Paso offer on-site propane gas refill
  • U-Haul and Tractor Supply locations fill standard RV cylinders (20-lb, 33-lb)
  • Propane dealers can fill larger onboard RV tanks that don't come off the vehicle (forklift-style POL connection)
