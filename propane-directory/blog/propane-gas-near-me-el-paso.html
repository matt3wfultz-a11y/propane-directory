Propane Gas Service Types Near El Paso

"Propane gas near me" can mean different things depending on your situation. Here's a breakdown of the main service categories available locally:

  • Cylinder refill (portable tanks): Bring your 20-lb, 33-lb, or larger cylinder to a local propane dealer and have it filled by an attendant. Best per-gallon price. Available at dealers, U-Haul locations, and select hardware stores across El Paso.
  • Tank exchange: Swap an empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at a self-serve cage — found at Walmart, Home Depot, Kroger, and most El Paso grocery stores. Open 24/7 but costs more per gallon, and tanks come only 75% full (15 lbs in a 20-lb cylinder).
  • Bulk propane delivery: For home heating, permanent tanks, commercial kitchens, or agricultural equipment, a local propane company delivers directly to your on-site tank. Lowest cost per gallon, but requires a permanent tank and a minimum order volume.
  • 1-lb and small camping cylinders: Single-use canisters for camping stoves, lanterns, and small torches. Available at Walmart, sporting goods stores, and hardware retailers across El Paso.

Refill vs Exchange: Which Gets You More Gas for Less?

When comparing propane gas options near El Paso, the math strongly favors refilling at a local dealer over exchange cages for most users:

OptionGas ReceivedTypical CostCost Per Gallon
Refill (local dealer, full fill)~4.7 gal (20 lbs)$16–$21$3.40–$4.50
Exchange cage (Blue Rhino / AmeriGas)~3.5 gal (15 lbs)$24–$28$4.80–$5.50

Exchange programs intentionally fill tanks to only 15 lbs — not the 20-lb capacity the cylinder can hold. That's 25% less gas, at a higher per-gallon rate. For a single tank, refilling at a local El Paso dealer saves you $7–$12. The exchange cage wins only when you need propane gas at 11 p.m. and no dealers are open.

Is Propane Gas Available 24/7 Near El Paso?

For cylinder refills, most El Paso propane dealers operate during standard business hours (typically Monday–Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Some U-Haul locations have extended hours. Exchange cages at retail stores are available 24/7 as long as they're stocked — Walmart, Home Depot, and grocery stores across El Paso carry Blue Rhino or AmeriGas cages in the parking lot or outside the entrance.

For emergency bulk delivery (a tank running dry in the middle of a cold night), most full-service propane companies in El Paso offer after-hours emergency service — expect a surcharge for off-hours calls. Keep your tank above 20% during cold months to avoid needing emergency delivery.
