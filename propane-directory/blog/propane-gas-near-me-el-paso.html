Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Local Suppliers & 2026 Prices

When you search "propane gas near me" in El Paso, you get results for everything from grill cylinder refills to whole-home delivery — and the prices vary more than you'd expect. Types of Propane Gas Service in El Paso

El Paso has a healthy mix of propane gas suppliers serving residential, commercial, and outdoor use. Here's what's available near you:

  • Cylinder refill stations: Dedicated propane dealers and select hardware retailers (U-Haul, Tractor Supply) fill cylinders of any size on-site. You pay by the gallon — typically $3.40–$4.50 in El Paso. This is the lowest-cost way to get propane gas.
  • Exchange cages: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and most grocery stores let you swap an empty 20-lb cylinder for a pre-filled one. Convenient but costs more per gallon, and tanks are only filled to 15 lbs — not a full 20-lb load.
  • Bulk residential delivery: For whole-home heating, water heaters, stoves, or generators, full-service propane companies (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, independent dealers) deliver directly to your tank. Typical El Paso bulk price: $2.80–$3.80 per gallon for volumes over 100 gallons.
  • Portable cylinder sales: Hardware stores, Walmart, and sporting goods stores sell pre-filled 1-lb disposable cylinders for camping stoves and lanterns — roughly $3–$5 each.

Who Uses Propane Gas in El Paso?

Propane is a versatile fuel used across El Paso for a wide range of purposes:

  • Outdoor grilling and patio heaters: The 20-lb cylinder is by far the most common size in El Paso, used for backyard grills, smokers, and patio heaters during the cooler months (October–March).
  • Residential heating and appliances: Many homes in the El Paso area — especially in Horizon City, Canutillo, Fabens, and rural West Texas — rely on propane for heating, water heaters, and cooking ranges.
  • RVs and camping: With easy access to Big Bend, White Sands, and the Guadalupe Mountains, El Paso is a frequent stop for RV travelers who need a propane fill before heading out.
  • Commercial and industrial use: Forklifts, temporary construction heaters, and food trucks in El Paso all commonly run on propane.

Tips for Finding Propane Gas Near You in El Paso

  • Call ahead for bulk pricing: Bulk propane rates in El Paso aren't always posted online. A quick call to Ferrellgas, AmeriGas, or a local independent dealer usually gets you a current per-gallon quote.
  • Check your tank's date: Propane cylinders must be recertified every 12 years. The manufacture date is stamped on the collar (e.g., "06 13" = June 2013). Most El Paso dealers won't refill an expired tank — check before you go.
