Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Where to Buy & What to Pay

November 20, 2026

When you search "propane gas near me" in El Paso, you're usually looking for one of two things: a place to get your cylinder filled or swapped, or a supplier for bulk propane gas delivery. El Paso is well-served for both — this guide walks through every option, what each costs in 2026, and how to find the closest location to wherever you are.

Ways to Get Propane Gas Near El Paso

Propane gas is sold in El Paso through three main channels:

  • Cylinder refill stations: You bring your tank; an attendant fills it by weight to its rated capacity. Lowest per-gallon cost, highest value. Available at propane dealers, U-Haul locations, and select hardware stores across El Paso.
  • Cylinder exchange cages: Pre-filled cylinders locked in a cage at the retailer entrance — swap your empty for a full one, 24/7. Blue Rhino and AmeriGas are the dominant brands. Available at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most gas stations across the city. Convenient but costs more per gallon, and tanks are only filled to 15 lbs (not the full 20-lb capacity).
  • Bulk propane delivery: A propane company delivers directly to your home or business tank. Economical for large-volume users — home heating, generators, agricultural equipment. Priced by the gallon with delivery minimums.

Refill vs Exchange: The Price Difference in El Paso

Exchange cages are everywhere in El Paso, but they come at a premium:

  • Exchange fills cylinders to only 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity. You're paying for a tank that's 25% less full than it could be.
  • Exchange runs $24–$28 per tank; refill runs $16–$21. That's a savings of $7–$12 per tank when you refill.
  • Over a full grilling or camping season (say, 8 fills), you'd save $56–$96 by using a refill station instead of exchanging.

The exception: if you need gas after hours or don't want to wait at a fill station, an exchange cage is fast and easy. For regular use, though, refilling saves significantly.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

Refill stations in El Paso are concentrated in a few location types:

  • U-Haul locations: Multiple El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill by weight. Good hours, reliable pricing, and usually no long wait.
  • Propane dealers: Local and national dealers (Ferrellgas, Superior Plus Propane) operate in El Paso and handle everything from grill cylinders to 100-lb residential tanks and bulk delivery.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: Outdoor fill station at select El Paso-area locations. Good option on the east side and Upper Valley.
  • RV service centers: Several near I-10 and Loop 375 cater to RV traffic and offer larger cylinder fills.

Exchange cages are at virtually every major grocery, hardware, and home improvement store in El Paso — including all Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and Albertsons locations.

Bulk Propane Gas Delivery Near El Paso

If you use propane for home heating, water heating, cooking, or generator backup, bulk delivery is the most cost-effective option in the long run. El Paso-area bulk propane suppliers typically offer:

  • Automatic delivery on a schedule based on your usage history
  • Will-call delivery when you request a fill
  • Emergency delivery for unexpected outages (may carry a surcharge)
  • Tank rental, purchase, or installation for new customers

Bulk pricing in El Paso runs $2.80–$3.80 per gallon as of 2026, depending on volume, contract terms, and the supplier. A 200-gallon fill at the low end runs around $560 — significantly less per gallon than any cylinder option.

Tips for Getting the Best Price on Propane Gas Near El Paso

  • Call ahead: Propane prices change with the market. Calling a dealer before you drive over takes 30 seconds and ensures you're not surprised at the pump.
  • Avoid peak seasons: Propane prices rise in winter (heating demand) and sometimes during heavy grilling holidays. Fill early if you can.
  • Choose refill over exchange: The math is consistent — a local refill costs less per gallon and delivers more gas per fill.
  • Check for deals: Some El Paso propane dealers offer loyalty programs, pre-pay plans, or bulk discounts. Ask when you call.
