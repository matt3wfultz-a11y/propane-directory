Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Suppliers, Prices & Delivery

September 4, 2026

If you're looking for propane gas near El Paso, TX, you have a strong mix of local dealers and regional suppliers to choose from. El Paso's location along the Texas-New Mexico supply corridor means propane prices here tend to run at or below the state average. This guide covers who supplies propane gas in the El Paso area, what you'll pay, and how to choose the right service for your needs.

Types of Propane Gas Service in El Paso

Propane gas in El Paso is available through several channels depending on what you need:

  • Cylinder refill: Bring a portable tank (20 lb, 33 lb, 100 lb) to a local dealer or service station and pay by the gallon. Best for grills, heaters, and outdoor appliances.
  • Tank exchange: Drop an empty cylinder at a retail cage (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas) and swap it for a pre-filled one. Convenient but more expensive per gallon.
  • Residential propane delivery: A dealer installs and fills a large stationary tank (250–1,000 gallon) at your home. Used for heating, water heaters, stoves, and whole-home propane systems — especially in areas of El Paso not served by natural gas lines.
  • Commercial and agricultural propane: Bulk delivery for businesses, farms, forklifts, and industrial uses. Priced at contract rates below retail.

Propane Gas for Homes Not on the Natural Gas Grid

Parts of El Paso — particularly in the far East Side, Lower Valley, and unincorporated areas of El Paso County — are not served by El Paso Electric's natural gas distribution lines. For homeowners in these areas, propane is the primary alternative for:

  • Space heating and central furnaces
  • Water heaters
  • Cooking ranges and ovens
  • Clothes dryers
  • Backup generators

Residential propane service involves leasing or purchasing a stationary tank (typically 250–500 gallons for a single-family home) and scheduling regular delivery. Most El Paso dealers offer automatic delivery programs that refill your tank before it runs low.

What to Ask a Propane Gas Supplier Before You Sign Up

  • Do you own or lease the tank? A dealer-owned tank usually ties you to that supplier. Owning your own tank gives you price flexibility.
  • What's the price per gallon today, and how does it change? Ask whether pricing is fixed, market-rate, or capped.
  • Is there a minimum delivery or annual fee? Some dealers charge fees if you don't use enough propane in a year.
  • What's the service area and delivery schedule? Confirm the dealer covers your zip code and can deliver within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Do you offer budget billing or price-lock programs? These can help even out costs over a year.
