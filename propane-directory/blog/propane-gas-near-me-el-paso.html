Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Local Suppliers & Current Prices

August 14, 2026

If you're looking for propane gas near El Paso, TX, the city has a well-developed network of suppliers — from walk-up cylinder refill stations to full-service residential delivery dealers. El Paso's location along major supply routes in the Texas–New Mexico corridor keeps local prices competitive. Here's everything you need to know about where to get propane gas near you and what it costs in 2026.

Types of Propane Gas Service Available Near El Paso

Propane gas comes in a few different service types, and the right one depends on how much you use and what equipment you have:

  • Cylinder refill (small tanks): Bring your 20 lb, 30 lb, or larger cylinder to a refill station and get it filled by the gallon. This is the most common option for grills, patio heaters, and small appliances. Best price per gallon of any walk-in option.
  • Tank exchange: Swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at a retail cage (Blue Rhino or AmeriGas). Fast and widely available across El Paso, but more expensive per gallon — tanks are only filled to about 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb rated capacity.
  • Residential bulk delivery: If you have a large above-ground or underground tank (120–500 gallons), a propane dealer delivers gas by truck on a route schedule. Best value per gallon and most practical for homes that rely on propane for heating, cooking, or water heating.
  • Commercial and industrial supply: El Paso's position as a border city means several dealers also handle forklift cylinders, construction site tanks, and agricultural supply for operations on both the Texas and New Mexico sides.

Where to Find Propane Gas Near El Paso

The El Paso propane directory lists 11 verified locations with addresses, phone numbers, and service types. In general, here's where to look:

  • Dedicated propane dealers: Local suppliers and national dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas bulk depots) fill cylinders of all sizes and offer residential delivery for large tanks. These typically have the lowest per-gallon prices.
  • U-Haul locations: Several El Paso U-Haul centers have propane refill stations on site. They fill standard cylinders (20 lb, 33 lb) at competitive rates and are convenient if you're already renting a truck or trailer.
  • Farm and ranch supply: Tractor Supply locations in the El Paso area offer outdoor propane refill stations during store hours — well-priced and reliable.
  • Retail exchange cages: Available at virtually every Walmart, Home Depot, Kroger, and most gas stations across El Paso. Good for emergencies or late-night needs; less economical for regular use.

Refill vs. Exchange: The Cost Gap in El Paso

Both options get you propane gas near El Paso, but the cost difference is significant. On a standard 20 lb grill cylinder:

  • Refill at a local dealer: You pay $16–$21 for a complete fill — all 4.7 gallons your cylinder can hold. Cost per gallon: $3.40–$4.50.
  • Exchange at a retail cage: You pay $24–$28 for a cylinder filled only to 15 lbs — about 3.5 gallons. Cost per gallon: $4.80–$5.50.

That's a savings of $7–$12 per fill when you refill instead of exchange. Over a full grilling season (4–6 fills), you're looking at $28–$72 in savings just by choosing a local refill station over an exchange cage. The extra few minutes of driving is almost always worth it.

El Paso's Propane Market: What Makes It Distinct

El Paso's desert climate means propane demand here is more evenly spread across the year than in most Texas cities. Grilling and outdoor entertaining drive summer demand; patio heaters, space heaters, and occasional home appliances drive winter use. The city also has a significant market for forklift and industrial propane given its commercial activity along the US–Mexico border.

Proximity to Las Cruces, NM — just 45 miles north — means some El Paso residents also have access to New Mexico dealers. See our Las Cruces propane directory if you're in the Canutillo or Vinton area and it's your nearest major city.

What to Bring When You Go for a Refill

  • Your cylinder — any size is fine at a dealer refill station

  • Payment (cash or card accepted at most El Paso dealers)

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get propane gas near El Paso?

Propane gas is available at dedicated propane dealers, U-Haul locations, Tractor Supply, and retail exchange cages (Walmart, Home Depot, most grocery stores) throughout El Paso. For a verified list of locations with addresses and phone numbers, use our El Paso propane directory.

How much does propane gas cost in El Paso?

Cylinder refills at local dealers run $3.40–$4.50 per gallon — or about $16–$21 for a standard 20 lb fill. Retail exchange programs cost effectively $4.80–$5.50/gal once you account for the reduced 15-lb fill. Residential bulk delivery runs $2.80–$3.60/gal for large tanks.

Does El Paso have home propane delivery?

Yes. Several full-service propane dealers in El Paso offer residential bulk delivery for customers with large above-ground or underground tanks. Ferrellgas and local independent dealers serve the El Paso metro, including areas west toward the New Mexico border. Call a local dealer to get set up with a tank and delivery schedule.

Is propane gas available year-round in El Paso?

Yes — El Paso's propane supply is stable year-round. The city's location along the Texas–New Mexico supply corridor means local dealers maintain consistent stock. Prices are typically slightly lower in spring and summer (April–August) when heating demand eases, and slightly higher in winter, but the swings are smaller than in northern states.
