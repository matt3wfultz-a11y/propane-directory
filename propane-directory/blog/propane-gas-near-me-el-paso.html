Propane Gas Near Me in El Paso, TX: Where to Buy & Current Prices

October 9, 2026

Searching for propane gas near El Paso, TX? Whether you need it for your backyard grill, a portable heater, an RV, or whole-home heating, El Paso has a solid network of propane gas suppliers — from local dealers and refill stations to bulk delivery providers. This guide covers where to buy propane gas near you, what you'll pay in 2026, and how to get the best price.

Where to Buy Propane Gas Near El Paso

El Paso has several ways to get propane gas depending on how much you need and how you use it:

  • Local propane dealers: Dedicated suppliers like Bob's Propane, DAE Propane, and AmeriGas offer refills, bulk delivery, and tank service for residential and commercial customers. These dealers give you the lowest per-gallon price — typically $3.40–$4.50/gal in 2026.
  • Refill stations (U-Haul, hardware stores): Several U-Haul locations and Tractor Supply stores in El Paso fill portable cylinders by weight. A reliable mid-range option for 20-lb and 33-lb tanks.
  • Exchange cages (Walmart, Home Depot, grocery stores): Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are at most major retailers across El Paso. Fast and convenient but more expensive per gallon — exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs rather than 20 lbs.
  • Bulk residential delivery: If your home runs on a large propane tank (250–1000 gal), a local or regional supplier delivers directly. Prices are negotiated and vary with market rates.

What Propane Gas Is Used For in El Paso

Propane is a versatile fuel used across El Paso homes, businesses, and outdoor settings:

  • Grilling and outdoor cooking: The most common use. A standard 20-lb tank fuels 18–20 hours of cooking on a medium grill.
  • Space heating and patio heaters: Propane heaters are popular in El Paso during cooler months (November–February) and for evening outdoor events.
  • Home heating and water heating: Homes without natural gas lines use propane for furnaces, boilers, water heaters, and stoves.
  • RV and camping: Propane powers RV stoves, furnaces, and refrigerators. Several El Paso-area RV parks and dealers offer propane gas service.
  • Construction and industrial: Propane torches, generators, and forklifts are widely used across El Paso's construction and warehouse sectors.

How to Get the Best Price on Propane Gas in El Paso

  • Refill over exchange: Refilling at a local dealer costs $7–$12 less per 20-lb tank than an exchange cage, and you get a full 20 lbs instead of 15 lbs.
  • Buy in the spring or summer: Residential delivery prices are typically lower in the off-peak season (April–September) when heating demand drops.
  • Ask about prepay programs: Some El Paso dealers offer lock-in pricing if you pay for a delivery in advance before the heating season.
  • Compare dealers: Prices vary by $0.30–$0.80/gal between El Paso suppliers. A quick call to two or three dealers before a large fill can save real money.
