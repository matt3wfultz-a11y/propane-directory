Where to Buy Propane Gas Near El Paso

El Paso has propane gas suppliers across the Lower Valley, Eastside, Westside, and Northeast corridors. Here's where to look for each service type:

Cylinder Refills

  • Local propane dealers: Dedicated dealers fill standard cylinders (20 lb to 100 lb) by weight on the spot. They typically offer the lowest per-gallon rate in El Paso — $3.40–$4.50/gal.
  • U-Haul locations: Multiple El Paso U-Haul centers fill 20 lb and 33 lb cylinders. No appointment needed; fill time is typically under 10 minutes.
  • Tractor Supply: The El Paso area Tractor Supply with an outdoor fill station handles standard cylinder refills during store hours.

Exchange Cages

  • Walmart: Most El Paso Walmart locations have Blue Rhino or AmeriGas exchange cages at the front entrance.
  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Both chains stock exchange cages near the contractor entrance or outdoor area.
  • H-E-B: El Paso H-E-B locations with garden centers typically carry exchange tanks.
  • Convenience stores: Circle K and Stripes locations around El Paso carry exchange tanks, useful for late-night or weekend needs.

Bulk Residential Delivery

If you have a 250-gallon or 500-gallon residential tank, local El Paso propane companies offer scheduled delivery and automatic refill programs. Contact dealers directly for current bulk pricing, which typically tracks $3.40–$4.50/gal for residential fills with a delivery minimum.

Why El Paso Propane Prices Run Competitive

El Paso's location on the Texas-New Mexico border gives it relatively direct access to propane supply infrastructure. The major pipeline and distribution networks that serve West Texas and southeastern New Mexico keep regional wholesale prices lower than in more isolated markets. That said, El Paso is also a border city — cross-border demand on the Juárez side adds some volume pressure, and the desert climate means both heating season (winter nights can dip below 30°F) and outdoor cooking season (long summers) create steady year-round demand.

Refill vs Exchange: Which Should You Choose Near El Paso?

The math is consistent regardless of which El Paso neighborhood you're in. Exchange programs fill 20 lb tanks to 15 lbs only, not the full 20-lb capacity. You're paying a higher rate for less gas:

  • Refill: ~4.7 gallons, $16–$21, $3.40–$4.50/gal
  • Exchange: ~3.5 gallons, $24–$28, $4.80–$5.50/gal

You save $7–$12 per tank by refilling when a local station is within reasonable driving distance. Exchange makes sense when you need gas right now and no refill station is open, or when your cylinder is too old or damaged to be refilled.
