What Is a Propane Refill Station?

A refill station fills your existing cylinder by weight — you pay by the gallon and get a complete fill up to the cylinder's rated capacity. This is different from an exchange program, which swaps your empty tank for a pre-filled one that's actually only filled to about 15 lbs (not the 20 lb maximum). Refill stations include:

  • Local propane dealers (independent suppliers, Ferrellgas depots, AmeriGas bulk locations)
  • U-Haul locations — many locations in Texas offer cylinder refill alongside truck rentals
  • Tractor Supply Co. — outdoor refill stations at farm and ranch supply stores statewide
  • Some RV dealerships and campgrounds — common along I-10, I-35, and I-45 corridors

What to Bring to a Propane Refill Station

  • Your cylinder: Any size — 20 lb, 30 lb, 33 lb, 40 lb, and 100 lb cylinders are filled at most Texas propane dealers.
  • Check the certification date: Look at the collar for a stamped month and year (e.g., "04 14" = April 2014). Cylinders must be recertified after 12 years — an out-of-date tank will be turned away. Recertification typically costs $5–$10 at dealers that offer the service.
  • Payment: Most Texas dealers accept credit and debit cards. Some independents are cash-preferred — call ahead if you're unsure.
  • Transportation: Carry the cylinder upright and secure. In a car, open the windows or trunk vent. In a truck bed, use a strap to prevent rolling.

When to Refill vs. When Exchange Makes Sense

Refilling is almost always the better deal, but exchange does have a place:

  • Choose refill when you want the best price, you have a working tank in good condition, and a refill station is within a reasonable drive.
  • Choose exchange when you're in an emergency with no refill stations nearby, you need propane outside of business hours, or your existing tank is old and needs replacement — exchange tanks are pre-inspected and current.

How much does a propane refill cost in Texas?

A standard 20 lb cylinder refill costs $14–$21 at Texas dealers — about $3.00–$4.50 per gallon. Exchange programs charge $24–$30 for a cylinder filled to only 15 lbs, an effective rate of $4.80–$5.50/gal. Refilling saves you $7–$12 per tank and gives you 25% more gas.

Can I refill a propane tank at Walmart in Texas?

Not directly — Walmart locations have exchange cages (Blue Rhino or AmeriGas) outside, not refill stations. You can swap your empty tank for a pre-filled one there, but it will only be filled to 15 lbs at a higher effective price. For a true refill, go to a propane dealer, U-Haul location, or Tractor Supply.

How long does a propane refill take in Texas?

Walk-up cylinder refills typically take 5–10 minutes at a Texas propane dealer or U-Haul location. The attendant weighs your tank, hooks it up to the fill line, fills to capacity, and you're done. No appointment needed — just pull up during business hours.
