Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Exchange & Refill

October 16, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Whether you need a brand-new cylinder, a filled exchange tank to swap for your empty one, or a refill station that tops off what you already own — El Paso has all three covered. This guide breaks down every option, what each costs, and where to find it.

Three Ways to Get a Propane Tank Near El Paso

When someone searches "propane tanks near me," they're usually after one of three things. Here's how each works in El Paso:

1. Buy a New Propane Tank

If you don't own a cylinder yet — or your old one is past recertification — you'll need to buy a new one. In El Paso, new 20-lb grill cylinders are sold at:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's — carry empty 20-lb cylinders, typically $30–$45 for the tank alone
  • Walmart and H-E-B — sell pre-filled 20-lb exchange tanks for $24–$28 (tank included in the first purchase)
  • Tractor Supply Co. — stocks a wider range including 33-lb and 40-lb cylinders
  • Local propane dealers — often the best source for larger tanks (100-lb cylinders, 420-lb tanks) and can fill them on the spot

Tip: If you're buying a new tank just to get propane, buying a pre-filled exchange tank at Walmart or H-E-B is often cheaper than buying an empty cylinder and paying a separate fill fee.

2. Exchange an Empty Tank

Exchange is the fastest option. Bring your empty 20-lb cylinder to the Blue Rhino or AmeriGas cage outside most El Paso retail stores, pay at the register, and walk out with a filled replacement. No wait, no appointment.

Exchange cages are available at virtually every major El Paso retailer: all Walmart locations, Home Depot, Lowe's, most H-E-B and Albertsons stores, and hundreds of gas stations across the Eastside, Westside, Northeast, and Lower Valley.

Important: Exchange tanks come filled to only 15 lbs — not the full 20-lb capacity. You pay for convenience, not a full tank.

3. Refill Your Existing Tank

A refill tops off your own cylinder to its full rated capacity — typically 20 lbs for a standard grill tank. It's consistently cheaper per gallon than exchange and gives you more gas per dollar. El Paso refill stations include U-Haul locations, Tractor Supply, dedicated propane dealers, and some hardware stores with outdoor fill stations.

Checking If Your Tank Is Still Good

Before you head out, check the date stamp on the collar of your propane cylinder. It reads as month/year — for example, "06 14" means it was manufactured or last certified in June 2014. Standard 20-lb cylinders must be recertified after 12 years, then again every 5 years. A tank stamped "06 14" was due for recertification in June 2026.

  • A refill station will refuse to fill an expired tank — they are legally required to
  • An exchange cage will accept an expired tank and swap it for a newer one — effectively upgrading your cylinder
  • For recertification options in El Paso, check local propane dealers; some do it on-site for $5–$10
