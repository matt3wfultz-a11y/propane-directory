Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Fill & Exchange

November 13, 2026

Whether you need a new propane tank, a refill for an existing cylinder, or a quick swap at an exchange cage, El Paso has you covered. This guide breaks down every option available in the El Paso metro area — where to go, what you'll pay, and which choice makes the most sense for your situation.

Three Ways to Get Propane Tanks Near El Paso

When you're searching for propane tanks near you in El Paso, you have three distinct options:

  • Buy a new cylinder: Purchase a brand-new empty tank and get it filled at a local dealer or refill station.
  • Refill your existing tank: Take your empty cylinder to a local propane dealer or refill station and pay only for the gas.
  • Exchange your empty tank: Swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at a retail exchange cage (Blue Rhino or AmeriGas).

For most regular users in El Paso, refilling is the best-value option. Here's how each one works.

Where to Buy Propane Tanks Near El Paso

New propane cylinders are sold at several locations throughout El Paso:

  • Home Depot & Lowe's: Both carry empty 20-lb cylinders in the garden/outdoor section. Expect to pay $35–$50 for a new OPD-valve cylinder. You can get it filled the same day at a local dealer.
  • Walmart Supercenter: Stocks empty 1-lb disposable canisters and occasionally 20-lb cylinders. The exchange cage outside also sells pre-filled 20-lb tanks (you'll pay the exchange price for a filled one).
  • Propane dealers: Local dealers like Ferrellgas and independent suppliers sell larger tanks — 33-lb, 40-lb, 100-lb, and above — that retail stores don't carry. If you need a 100-lb cylinder for a commercial grill or residential use, a dealer is your only local option.
  • Tractor Supply: Good source for 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders, plus propane accessories. Many El Paso-area Tractor Supply stores also offer refill service.

Where to Refill Propane Tanks Near El Paso

Refilling is the most cost-effective way to get propane for your existing cylinder. El Paso refill locations include:

  • Local propane dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas bulk dealers, independent suppliers) — lowest per-gallon price, fill any size tank
  • U-Haul locations — several El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill by weight for 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders
  • Tractor Supply — outdoor refill station, typically at the lower end of the retail price range
  • Some hardware stores — select Ace Hardware and True Value locations in El Paso offer refill service alongside exchange cages

Checking Your Tank Before You Go

Before taking a tank in for a refill or exchange, check these two things:

  • Expiration date: Look at the collar stamp (e.g., "06 14" = June 2014 manufacture date, recertification due June 2026). Most El Paso refill dealers won't fill a cylinder that's past its certification date. If yours is expired, an exchange cage will accept it and swap it for a newer tank.
  • Tank condition: Rusty, dented, or damaged cylinders may be refused. If your tank looks rough, an exchange is a good opportunity for a free upgrade to a newer cylinder.
