Where to Find Propane Tanks Near You in El Paso

The most common places to get a propane tank in or near El Paso:

  • Exchange cages at grocery and home improvement stores: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are found at most Walmart, Home Depot, and Lowe's locations in the El Paso metro. You drop off an empty tank and pick up a pre-filled one. Fast, but the tank you receive is only filled to 15 lbs — not the full 20-lb capacity.
  • Hardware stores selling new cylinders: Tractor Supply, Home Depot, and Walmart all carry new OPD-valve 20-lb cylinders for purchase. These come empty; you'll need a refill station to fill them. Prices typically run $30–$45 for a new unfilled tank.
  • Dedicated propane dealers: Local suppliers like West Texas Propane, Bob's Propane, DAE Propane, and Ferrellgas can sell you a tank and fill it in the same visit, often at a lower combined cost than buying retail and exchanging elsewhere.
  • U-Haul locations: Several El Paso U-Haul centers sell propane cylinders and offer on-site refill service for 20-lb and 33-lb tanks.
  • RV and outdoor supply stores: For larger tanks (40-lb, 100-lb), RV dealers and outdoor equipment retailers along I-10 stock a wider range of cylinder sizes than big-box stores.

What to Check Before Using a Propane Tank

Before you buy, exchange, or refill, it's worth doing a quick inspection:

  • Expiration date: Propane tanks carry a manufacture date stamped on the collar (e.g., "06 14" = June 2014). New tanks are good for 12 years; after that they need recertification or replacement. A tank stamped before July 2014 is now past its first certification period.
  • OPD valve: All cylinders 4–40 lbs must have an OPD (Overfill Prevention Device) valve — the triangular handwheel is the giveaway. Older tanks without OPD valves won't be filled at any reputable dealer.
  • Rust and damage: Surface rust is usually cosmetic; deep pitting, dents near the valve, or heavy corrosion are reasons to retire the tank.
  • Valve condition: The valve should turn smoothly and seat fully when closed. A leaking or damaged valve needs repair before filling.

Exchange vs. Buy + Refill: Which Makes More Sense?

If you grill or use propane appliances regularly, buying a 20-lb tank once and refilling it locally pays for itself within the first two to three refills:

  • Exchange cage: ~$26 per visit, 15 lbs of gas each time = roughly $1.73 per lb
  • Local refill: ~$18 per visit, 20 lbs of gas each time = roughly $0.90 per lb
  • Breakeven on a new tank purchase: approximately 3 refill cycles vs. what you'd have spent at exchange cages

The only scenario where exchange genuinely wins: you're traveling, your tank is very old, or you need gas immediately and there's no refill station near you.
