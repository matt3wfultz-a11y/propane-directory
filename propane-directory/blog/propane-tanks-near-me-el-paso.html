Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill, or Exchange

July 24, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso? Whether you need to buy a new cylinder, refill the one you have, or swap it at an exchange cage, El Paso has options across the entire metro — from the Westside to the Lower Valley. This guide breaks down all three options, what you'll pay in 2026, and where to find the nearest location.

Option 1: Buy a New Propane Tank in El Paso

If you don't own a tank yet, you can purchase one at several retailers across El Paso:

  • Hardware and home improvement stores: Home Depot and Ace Hardware carry standard 20-lb grill cylinders and some larger sizes. Expect to pay $35–$55 for an empty tank.
  • Tractor Supply: Carries a wider range of cylinder sizes, including 40-lb and 100-lb tanks for residential use.
  • Local propane dealers: Bob's Propane, B&B Propane, and other independent dealers in El Paso sell new cylinders and can fill them on the spot. This is usually the most economical path — you buy the tank and leave with a full one.
  • Exchange programs: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas cages at Walmart, Home Depot, and grocery stores across El Paso let you pick up a pre-filled 20-lb tank for $24–$28. The tank is only filled to about 15 lbs, but you walk away with gas immediately.

Option 2: Refill Your Existing Tank in El Paso

If you already own a cylinder, a local refill is the cheapest way to get propane. El Paso has multiple refill stations across the city:

  • Dedicated propane dealers (Bob's Propane, B&B Propane, DAE Propane, West Texas Propane) — fill tanks by weight to the full rated capacity. Best per-gallon price in the city.
  • U-Haul locations — several El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill by weight for standard cylinders.
  • Tractor Supply — outdoor propane fill station available at select El Paso locations.

For a complete list with addresses and phone numbers, see the El Paso propane directory.

Option 3: Exchange at a Cage Near You

Propane exchange cages from Blue Rhino and AmeriGas are the most widely available option in El Paso — you'll find them at most Walmart Supercenter, Home Depot, and H-E-B locations throughout the city. Just bring your empty tank, hand it to the attendant or use the self-service cage, and take a pre-filled one.

Exchange is convenient but more expensive per gallon. Exchange tanks are only filled to approximately 15 lbs (not the full 20-lb capacity), so you're paying more for less gas.

Before You Go: What to Check on Your Tank

  • Expiration date: Look for a date stamp on the collar (e.g., "06 14" = manufactured June 2014). Tanks must be recertified 12 years after manufacture and again every 5 years after that. An expired tank cannot be legally refilled until recertified.
  • Valve condition: Make sure the OPD valve (the triangular handwheel) is intact. Dealers can refuse tanks with damaged valves.
  • Rust and dents: Surface rust is usually fine; deep pitting or large dents are grounds for rejection.
