Where to Buy Propane Tanks in El Paso

For portable cylinders (20 lb and under), El Paso has plenty of retail options:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Sell new 20-lb cylinders in store, and most locations have an exchange cage outside for filled tanks.
  • Walmart: Carries new empty cylinders and operates AmeriGas exchange cages near the entrance of most El Paso stores.
  • Tractor Supply: A good source for 20-lb and 40-lb cylinders, often at competitive prices. Most El Paso locations also offer propane refill service.
  • Sporting goods stores (Academy, Bass Pro Shops): Stock 1-lb disposable cylinders and sometimes 20-lb refillables for camping use.
  • Local propane dealers: For 100-lb cylinders and larger, your best bet is a dedicated propane company. They can sell a tank outright, lease it to you, or install a large residential tank.

Renting vs. Buying Large Propane Tanks

For residential tanks (250 gallons and up), most El Paso propane suppliers offer a lease or rental model rather than outright sale. Here's how it typically works:

  • Leased tank: The supplier owns the tank, installs it at your property, and charges an annual lease fee (often $50–$150/year). You're typically locked into buying propane from that supplier.
  • Owned tank: You purchase the tank outright (usually $800–$2,500 depending on size) and can shop around for the best propane price from any local supplier. Higher upfront cost, but more flexibility long-term.

If you're buying a home that already has a propane tank, check whether it's leased or owned — it affects which supplier you can use.

Refilling Your Propane Tank in El Paso

Once you have a tank, refilling it locally is almost always cheaper than an exchange. Current refill prices in El Paso for a standard 20-lb cylinder run $16–$21 (roughly $3.40–$4.50 per gallon), compared to $24–$28 for an exchange cage — and exchange cages only fill to 15 lbs, not the full 20 lbs.

Refill locations near El Paso include dedicated propane dealers, U-Haul centers, and select Tractor Supply stores. For a full list with addresses and phone numbers, see our El Paso propane directory.

Propane Tank Safety & Age Rules

Before bringing an old tank in for a refill, check the date stamp on the collar. Propane cylinders must be recertified 12 years after manufacture, then every 5 years after that. A stamp reading "06 14" means the tank was made in June 2014 — recertification is due June 2026. Most El Paso dealers will not fill an expired tank; some offer on-site recertification.
