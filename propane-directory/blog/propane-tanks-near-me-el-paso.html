Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Fill & Exchange

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Whether you need to buy a new cylinder, get an existing one filled, or swap it at an exchange cage, El Paso has convenient options across the city. Where to Find Propane Tanks Near El Paso

El Paso has no shortage of places to get a propane tank — the options split into three main categories:

  • Buy a new empty tank: Home Depot, Lowe's, and Walmart all carry standard 20-lb cylinders in the $35–$55 range. Tractor Supply carries larger cylinders (33 lb, 40 lb) suited for RVs, generators, and high-output heaters. Local propane dealers sell all sizes and can fill the tank the same day.
  • Refill your existing tank: Local propane dealers, U-Haul locations, and some hardware stores fill your cylinder by weight — topping it to full capacity. This is always cheaper per gallon than an exchange. See the full list in our El Paso propane directory.
  • Exchange your empty tank: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are located outside Walmart, Home Depot, Walgreens, and most major grocery stores across El Paso. You swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one — no appointment, 24/7 availability. The downside: exchange tanks are filled to only 15 lbs (not the full 20-lb capacity), and the per-gallon price is higher.

Tank Condition: What to Check Before You Refill

Before bringing a cylinder to a refill station or an exchange cage, check these things:

  • Expiration date: Propane cylinders must be recertified 12 years after their manufacture date, then every 5 years after that. Look for a date stamp on the collar — for example, "06 14" means June 2014, making it due for recertification by June 2026. An expired tank won't be filled at most locations.
  • Rust or damage: Surface rust on the cylinder exterior, dents, or visible valve damage are reasons a dealer may refuse to fill the tank. Minor cosmetic rust on the bottom ring is typically okay; rust on the body or neck is not.
  • OPD valve: Any cylinder sold after 1998 should have an Overfill Protection Device (OPD) — identifiable by a triangular handwheel on the valve. Exchange programs generally won't accept tanks without OPD valves.

If your cylinder is expired or damaged, local dealers can sell you a new one and fill it on the spot. For recertification questions, see our guide: Propane Tank Recertification: When Does Your Tank Expire?
