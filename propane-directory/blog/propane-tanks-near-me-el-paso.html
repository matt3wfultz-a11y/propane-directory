Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill & Exchange

November 20, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Whether you need to buy a brand-new cylinder, get an empty one refilled, or swap at a local exchange cage, El Paso has convenient options across every part of the city. This guide covers where to find propane tanks near you, what they cost, and how to choose the right size and service for your needs.

Where to Buy Propane Tanks Near El Paso

If you need a new propane cylinder, these are your best bets in the El Paso area:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Both carry standard 20-lb grill cylinders (typically $35–$45 empty) and can fill or exchange them on the spot at the customer service desk or outdoor cage.
  • Walmart: Sells new 20-lb tanks in the sporting goods and outdoor sections, usually $35–$50 empty. Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are also available outside most El Paso Walmart locations.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: Good source for larger cylinders — 33-lb, 40-lb, and 100-lb tanks used for RVs, generators, and farm equipment. Propane refill service is also available at select locations.
  • Local propane dealers: Dealers like Ferrellgas and Superior Plus Propane sell cylinders in all sizes and fill them on-site. If you're buying new and want it filled immediately, a dealer is your most efficient stop.
  • Academy Sports: Carries grill-size (20-lb) cylinders, especially in camping and grilling season. Good fallback if box stores are out of stock.

Getting Your Propane Tank Filled in El Paso

Once you have a tank, you have two ways to get propane into it:

Option 1: Refill at a Local Station

Bring your empty cylinder to a propane refill station and an attendant fills it to its rated capacity. This is the most cost-effective option in El Paso — a 20-lb refill runs $16–$21, compared to $24–$28 for an exchange. You also get a full fill (20 lbs) instead of the partial fill you receive with exchange programs.

Refill locations in El Paso include U-Haul centers, select Tractor Supply locations, local propane dealers, and some RV service centers. See our El Paso propane directory for a complete, verified list with addresses and phone numbers.

Option 2: Tank Exchange

Swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at an exchange cage. Blue Rhino and AmeriGas cages are located outside Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most El Paso grocery and hardware stores. Exchange is fast and available 24/7, but there are trade-offs:

  • Exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity
  • Cost runs $24–$28 per swap — more expensive per gallon than a refill
  • You lose your existing tank (you get back a different one, which may be older)

Exchange is a good fit if you need propane urgently outside of business hours or don't have time to wait for a fill. For regular use, a refill saves you $7–$12 per tank.

Tank Recertification: Know Your Expiration Date

Propane cylinders have a 12-year service life from the manufacture date stamped on the collar. After 12 years, the tank must be recertified (re-qualified) before a dealer will refill it. Look for a stamp like "06 14" on the collar — that means June 2014, so the tank was due for recertification in June 2026.

A recertified tank gets a new 5-year certification. If your tank is expired, most El Paso propane dealers can recertify it for a small fee ($10–$15), or you can trade it in toward a new one through an exchange program.
