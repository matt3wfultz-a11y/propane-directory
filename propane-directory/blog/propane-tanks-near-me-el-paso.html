Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Where to Buy, Refill & Exchange

October 2, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso? Whether you need to buy a brand-new cylinder, refill an empty tank, or swap it at an exchange cage, El Paso has options spread across every part of the city — from the Westside to the Lower Valley to Northeast El Paso. This guide covers every service type, what you'll pay, and how to find the nearest location fast.

What "Propane Tanks Near Me" Actually Means

Most people searching for propane tanks nearby want one of three things:

  • Buy a new tank: You need a cylinder for the first time (a new grill, a patio heater, a camper).
  • Refill an existing tank: Your cylinder is empty or running low and you want to pay per gallon to top it off.
  • Exchange an empty tank: You want the quick swap — drop off your empty cylinder and pick up a pre-filled one at a cage outside a retailer.

All three services are available throughout El Paso, but the costs vary significantly. Here's what to expect for each.

Where to Buy New Propane Tanks in El Paso

New propane cylinders are available at hardware stores, outdoor retailers, and most home improvement stores across El Paso:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Both carry standard 20-lb (grill) cylinders, usually priced around $35–$45 for the tank itself (not including propane). Some locations also stock 30-lb and 40-lb cylinders.
  • Walmart: Stocks new 20-lb cylinders in the sporting goods or outdoor section, typically $30–$40. Exchange cages at the entrance handle refills.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: Carries a wider range of cylinder sizes, including 40-lb and 100-lb tanks. Good option if you need a larger residential cylinder.
  • Local propane dealers: Ferrellgas and independent dealers will sell you a new cylinder and fill it on the spot — often the most economical route if you're buying your first tank.

Where to Refill Propane Tanks in El Paso

Refilling your existing cylinder is almost always the cheapest option. El Paso refill stations include:

  • Dedicated propane dealers: Independent suppliers and chain dealers like Ferrellgas fill tanks of all sizes by weight. Prices run $3.40–$4.50 per gallon — the lowest you'll find.
  • U-Haul centers: Multiple El Paso U-Haul locations fill 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders at competitive rates.
  • Tractor Supply: Outdoor fill stations at select locations service standard grill cylinders.
  • RV dealers near I-10: If you need a 30-lb or larger tank filled, some RV service centers along the interstate offer propane service.

Where to Exchange Propane Tanks in El Paso

Exchange cages (Blue Rhino and AmeriGas brands) are the most convenient option — no waiting, just swap and go. You'll find exchange cages at:

  • Walmart locations across El Paso
  • Home Depot and Lowe's garden center entrances
  • Kroger / Fred Meyer grocery stores
  • Most major gas stations and convenience stores

What to Know Before You Go

  • Check your tank's date stamp: Look at the collar (the ring below the valve). A stamp like "06 14" means the tank was manufactured June 2014. Propane tanks must be recertified or replaced after 12 years — a 2014 tank was due in 2026.
  • Transport upright: Always carry propane cylinders upright in a vehicle, with the valve closed. Crack a window or use a truck bed.
  • Dealers fill by weight: A reputable refill station weighs your cylinder before and after to ensure you get a full fill. If they don't weigh it, find another location.
