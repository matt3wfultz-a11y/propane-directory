Searching for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Whether you need a fresh 20-lb grill cylinder, a 100-lb tank for a heater, or a large residential tank for your home, El Paso has multiple options for buying, renting, and exchanging propane tanks. Where to Find Propane Tanks Near You in El Paso

The right place to go depends on what size tank you need:

  • Portable cylinders (1 lb – 100 lb): Available for purchase at Walmart, Home Depot, ACE Hardware, and Tractor Supply locations across El Paso. Local propane dealers also stock cylinders and can fill or sell them on the spot.
  • Exchange cages: Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages are located outside most major grocery stores and Walmarts in El Paso. You bring an empty 20-lb cylinder and swap it for a pre-filled one.
  • Large residential tanks (250 gal – 1000 gal): These are typically leased from a propane supplier rather than purchased. Local and national dealers (AmeriGas, Ferrellgas, Superior Plus) serve the El Paso metro area.

Tank Age and Recertification

Propane cylinders have a 12-year lifespan from their manufacture date before they need recertification. Look at the collar (the metal ring near the valve) for a stamped date — for example, "08 14" means August 2014. A tank stamped before October 2014 is now past its 12-year window and must be recertified before a dealer will refill it.

Recertification typically costs $5–$15 and adds 5 years of service life. Several El Paso propane dealers offer on-site recertification — call ahead to confirm. If a tank is too old to recertify economically, a new 20-lb cylinder at $25–$45 is often the better move.
