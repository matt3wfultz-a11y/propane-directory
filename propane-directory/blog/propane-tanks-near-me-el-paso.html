Where to Buy a New Propane Tank in El Paso

New empty cylinders are widely available across the city. You don't need to buy from a specialty propane dealer — most major retailers carry standard 20-lb cylinders:

  • Home Depot & Lowe's — stock empty 20-lb cylinders in the outdoor/garden department, typically $35–$50. Also sell smaller 1-lb and 16.4-oz camping cylinders.
  • Walmart — carries 20-lb cylinders (seasonal availability) and smaller sizes year-round. Often the most competitively priced for basic cylinders.
  • Tractor Supply Co. — sells 20-lb, 30-lb, and 40-lb cylinders. Useful if you need a larger-than-standard size. Also offers on-site refill service at most El Paso area locations.
  • Local propane dealers — often sell cylinders directly, sometimes at a discount if you're also paying for a fill. Ask when you arrive for a refill.
  • Exchange cage (first-time purchase) — if you don't own a cylinder, picking up a pre-filled Blue Rhino or AmeriGas tank from a retail cage is a fast way to get started. You're essentially buying a filled tank at the exchange price ($24–$28) and now own the cylinder for future refills.

Where to Exchange a Propane Tank in El Paso

Exchange cages are at virtually every major retail location in El Paso. You'll find Blue Rhino and AmeriGas cages outside:

  • Walmart Supercenter — Eastside, Westside, Northeast, and Lower Valley locations
  • Home Depot and Lowe's — all El Paso area stores
  • H-E-B, Albertsons, and most grocery chains
  • Circle K, Stripes, Valero, and major gas station chains throughout the metro

Exchange tanks are filled to 15 lbs only — not the full 20-lb capacity. At $24–$28 per swap, the effective cost per gallon is $4.80–$5.50, significantly more than a local refill. But exchange is the right call when it's late, you're in a rush, or your current tank is past its certification date and a dealer won't fill it.

What to Know Before You Go

  • Check your tank's date stamp: Look on the collar for a stamped date (e.g., "04 14" = April 2014). Tanks expire 12 years from manufacture. An April 2014 tank hit its expiration in April 2026 — dealers will refuse to fill it. Exchange programs will accept it and swap it for a newer cylinder.
  • Cylinder condition: Tanks with significant rust, dents at the seams, or damaged valves may be refused. A visual check before driving to a dealer saves a wasted trip.
  • Transport upright: Always carry propane cylinders upright in your vehicle — in a truck bed or with the car windows cracked. Never transport a filled tank in a sealed trunk.
  • Pay method: Most El Paso dealers accept cash and card. Exchange cages are paid at the store register or a self-serve kiosk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy a propane tank near El Paso, TX?

New 20-lb cylinders are available at Home Depot, Lowe's, Walmart, and Tractor Supply locations across El Paso, typically priced $30–$50 empty. Local propane dealers also sell cylinders and will fill them on the same visit. If you want a ready-to-use tank immediately, exchange cages at any Walmart or Home Depot offer pre-filled cylinders for $24–$28.

Is it cheaper to refill or exchange a propane tank?

Refilling is significantly cheaper. El Paso refill stations charge $3.40–$4.50 per gallon for a full 20-lb fill ($16–$21 total). Exchange programs charge $24–$28 for only 15 lbs — an effective rate of $4.80–$5.50 per gallon. The savings add up to $7–$12 per fill in favor of refilling.

Can I get a propane tank filled same day in El Paso?

Yes. Propane dealers, U-Haul locations, and Tractor Supply fill cylinders on the spot — no appointment needed. Exchange cages are self-serve and available 24/7 at most El Paso retail locations.

How do I know if my propane tank is expired?

Find the date stamp on your cylinder's collar (e.g., "06 14" = June 2014). The tank is valid for 12 years from that date. After 12 years it needs recertification before a dealer will refill it. If recertification isn't practical, exchange programs will accept the expired tank and swap it for a newer one.
