Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Fill & Exchange

Whether you need a new propane tank, a refill on a cylinder you already own, or a quick swap at an exchange cage, El Paso has you covered. What Kind of Propane Tank Do You Need?

Before heading out, it helps to know which situation you're in:

  • You need a new tank: Hardware stores, propane dealers, and big-box retailers sell new cylinders. A standard 20-lb grill cylinder runs $30–$50 new.
  • You have an empty tank and want it filled: Refill stations fill your existing cylinder by weight. This is the cheapest way to get propane per gallon.
  • You want a quick swap: Exchange cages let you hand in your empty tank and walk out with a pre-filled replacement. Convenient, but more expensive per gallon and tanks are only filled to 15 lbs, not 20.

Checking Your Tank Before You Go

A few things to check before taking your cylinder to be filled:

  • Expiration date: Look at the collar stamp (e.g. "09 14" = September 2014). Tanks more than 12 years old need recertification before a dealer will fill them — or you can swap through an exchange program, which will accept old tanks and replace them with a newer one
  • Valve and OPD: All cylinders made after 1998 should have an OPD (overfill prevention device) valve. If your tank's valve is a three-pronged handwheel, it predates OPD and most dealers won't fill it
  • Visible damage: Rust, dents, or damaged foot rings are grounds for refusal at a refill station
