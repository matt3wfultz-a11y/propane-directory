Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill & Exchange

October 30, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso? Whether you need a brand-new cylinder, a refill for an existing tank, or a quick cage exchange before your next cookout, El Paso has options in every part of the city. This guide breaks down where to find tanks, what they cost, and which service — buy, refill, or exchange — gives you the most value.

What "Propane Tanks Near Me" Usually Means

The search phrase covers three different needs, each with a different answer:

  • Buying a new tank: If you don't own a cylinder yet, you need to purchase one first. New 20-lb cylinders retail for $35–$55 at hardware stores and propane dealers.
  • Refilling an existing tank: You already own a cylinder and want it topped off. This is the most cost-effective option — typically $16–$21 for a 20-lb tank in El Paso.
  • Exchanging a tank: You swap your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one at a cage kiosk (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas). Fast and convenient, but costs more per gallon and fills to only 15 lbs instead of 20.

Where to Find Propane Tanks in El Paso

Buy a New Tank

New propane cylinders are available at hardware and home improvement stores across El Paso. Home Depot, Walmart, and Tractor Supply carry 20-lb cylinders at the service desk or garden center. Local propane dealers also sell new tanks of all sizes, from 20-lb grill cylinders up to 100-lb and larger residential tanks.

Refill Stations

For the best price on a fill, head to a dedicated refill station. El Paso has propane refill locations on the Westside, Northeast, and in the Lower Valley. Dedicated dealers like Ferrellgas and AmeriGas bulk sites fill by weight and charge the lowest per-gallon rates. U-Haul locations across the city also offer propane refill for standard cylinders.

See our full list of verified refill stations: El Paso propane directory.

Exchange Cages

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages sit outside most El Paso Walmart, Home Depot, H-E-B, and Lowe's locations. Open 24/7 (the cage accepts payment by card), exchanges take under two minutes. Just remember: exchange tanks are filled to only 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity.

Which Option Is Right for You?

  • Buy new if you don't own a cylinder at all, or if your existing tank has failed recertification.
  • Refill if you want the most gas for the lowest cost. Saves $7–$12 per cycle compared to exchange.
  • Exchange if convenience is the priority — it's fast, no wait, no scales. Just know you're getting 25% less gas than a refill.

Tank Safety Before You Fill or Exchange

Before taking a tank to any El Paso refill station or exchange cage, do a quick check:

  • Check the collar date stamp. Tanks more than 12 years old need recertification — a dealer can't legally fill them.
  • Look for dents, rust, or damage to the valve. Damaged tanks are rejected at every station.
  • Transport upright in an open truck bed or with the vehicle windows cracked.
