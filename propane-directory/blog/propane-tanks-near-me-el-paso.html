Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Fill & Exchange

August 14, 2026

Whether you need a new propane tank, a refill for your existing cylinder, or an empty-for-full exchange, El Paso has options spread across every part of the city. This guide covers where to find propane tanks near you in El Paso, what sizes are available, what you'll pay, and which option makes the most sense for your situation.

Your Three Options: Buy, Refill, or Exchange

When people search for "propane tanks near me," they're usually trying to do one of three things — and the right answer depends on which one applies to you:

  • Buy a new tank: If you don't own a cylinder yet, you'll need to purchase one. Hardware stores and Walmart carry standard 20 lb and 30 lb cylinders. Full-service dealers can set you up with large residential tanks.
  • Refill an existing tank: If your cylinder is empty or low, refilling it at a local propane dealer is the cheapest option — you pay by the gallon for a full fill. This is almost always the best value.
  • Exchange an empty tank: You drop off your empty tank and pick up a pre-filled one at a cage outside a store. It's fast and available at dozens of El Paso locations, but you pay more per gallon and get less propane (about 15 lbs instead of a full 20).

Where to Find Propane Tanks Near You in El Paso

Buy a New Cylinder

New 20 lb propane cylinders are available at most major retailers across El Paso:

  • Walmart: Multiple locations citywide carry empty 20 lb and 30 lb cylinders in the outdoor/sporting goods section. Exchange cages outside also let you pick up a pre-filled tank.
  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Both carry new cylinders and have exchange cages at the entrance. Selection typically includes 20 lb and occasionally 30 lb tanks.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: Stocks a wider range of cylinder sizes and has on-site refill service at the outdoor fill station.
  • Independent propane dealers: The best source for larger cylinders (40 lb, 100 lb) and for large residential tanks (120–500 gallons). Dealers in El Paso can often sell you a tank outright or set up a lease agreement for a large above-ground unit.

Refill an Existing Tank

Refill stations fill your cylinder by weight — you get a complete fill to rated capacity, paying only for the propane you receive. For the full list of verified refill locations in El Paso, see our El Paso propane directory. Common station types include:

  • Local propane dealers (Ferrellgas, AmeriGas depots, independents): Lowest per-gallon price, fill all sizes
  • U-Haul locations: Multiple El Paso locations offer propane refill; reliable for 20 lb and 33 lb cylinders
  • Tractor Supply: Outdoor refill station, well-priced, available during store hours

Exchange an Empty Tank

Exchange cages are operated by Blue Rhino and AmeriGas at most Walmart, Home Depot, grocery, and gas station locations across El Paso. You pay a flat fee and walk away with a pre-filled (15 lb, not 20 lb) tank in minutes. Convenient — but the most expensive option per gallon.

What to Know Before You Go

  • Check your tank's expiration date: Look at the collar of your cylinder for a stamped date (e.g., "06 14" = manufactured June 2014). DOT cylinders must be recertified or replaced after 12 years. An expired tank will be refused at most refill stations.
  • Transport tanks upright: Always carry propane cylinders upright and secured — ideally in the truck bed or with your vehicle windows cracked open.
  • Call ahead for hours: Dealer refill stations are open during business hours; they do not operate 24/7. Exchange cages at big-box stores are typically accessible whenever the store is open.
  • Larger tanks require scheduled delivery: Residential tanks over 100 lbs are filled by a propane delivery truck on a route schedule, not on demand at a station.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy a propane tank near El Paso?

New 20 lb cylinders are available at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and Tractor Supply across El Paso. For larger tanks (40 lb, 100 lb, residential), contact a full-service propane dealer directly — they stock a broader range of sizes and can also set up large above-ground tanks for home delivery.

How much does a 20 lb propane tank cost in El Paso?

An empty 20 lb cylinder costs $30–$50 at hardware stores and Walmart. Once purchased, refilling it at a local dealer runs $16–$21 per fill (about 4.7 gallons at $3.40–$4.50/gal). A pre-filled exchange tank costs $24–$28 but only contains 15 lbs of propane — less gas for more money.

Can I get a propane tank refilled same-day in El Paso?

Yes — most El Paso refill stations are walk-up or drive-up service. Bring your cylinder, pull up to the fill station, and you'll be out in minutes. Call ahead to confirm hours, especially on weekends or holidays.

What's the largest propane tank I can get near El Paso?

Full-service dealers in the El Paso metro area handle residential delivery tanks up to 500 gallons or larger for commercial accounts. Large tanks are typically leased from the dealer rather than purchased outright. Delivery is scheduled on a route — you won't pick these up at a station.
