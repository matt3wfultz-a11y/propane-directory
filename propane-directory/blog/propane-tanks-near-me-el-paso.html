Is Your Tank Too Old to Fill?

Propane cylinders must be recertified every 12 years. Check the collar of your tank for a stamped date (e.g., "06 14" means June 2014, so recertification was due June 2026). If your tank is past due, most El Paso refill dealers will turn it away — but a retail exchange program will usually accept it as a trade-in and hand you a recertified replacement instead. Some local dealers also offer recertification service if you'd rather keep your original cylinder. For the full rundown, see Propane Tank Recertification: When Does Your Tank Expire?

Frequently Asked Questions

What size propane tank do I need?

For a backyard grill or patio heater, a 20-lb cylinder is standard. RVs, campers, and forklifts often use 30-lb or 33-lb tanks. Large patio heaters and small generators may call for a 40-lb cylinder, while home backup generators commonly use 100-lb tanks. Whole-home heating requires a fixed 250-gallon or larger tank filled by bulk delivery.

How much does a new propane tank cost in El Paso?

A new empty 20-lb cylinder typically costs $35–$50 at Home Depot, Lowe's, or Walmart, and $40–$60 at a local propane dealer. Larger 30-lb and 40-lb cylinders run roughly $50–$80 new — and remember a new tank arrives empty, so add the cost of a first fill.

Can I get a propane tank filled that I bought somewhere else?

Yes. As long as your cylinder has a standard OPD valve and is within its 12-year recertification window, any refill dealer in El Paso can fill it regardless of where you bought it or what brand is on the collar.

Where can I exchange a propane tank in El Paso?

Exchange cages are available at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, most El Paso grocery stores, and many gas stations citywide. They're convenient but fill to only 15 lbs instead of a full 20-lb capacity, so a refill at a local dealer is usually the better deal.
