Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Where to Buy, Swap & Refill

September 11, 2026

Looking for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Whether you need a brand-new cylinder, a quick exchange swap before a weekend cookout, or a local dealer who will refill your existing tank for less, El Paso has solid options across the city. This guide covers every way to get a propane tank near you — with 2026 prices so you know what to expect before you go.

Types of Propane Tanks Available Near El Paso

Propane tanks come in several standard sizes. Knowing which one you need before you shop saves time and money:

  • 20-lb cylinder (standard grill tank): The most common size in El Paso — used for gas grills, portable heaters, and small fire pits. Holds approximately 4.7 gallons when full. Available everywhere from Walmart to local dealers.
  • 33-lb cylinder (forklift / RV): Holds about 7.7 gallons. Used for larger RVs, commercial fryers, and forklifts. Less common at big-box stores; available at propane dealers and Tractor Supply.
  • 40-lb cylinder (large patio / generator): Holds roughly 9.4 gallons. A good choice for standby generators and high-output patio heaters. Available at most propane dealers.
  • 100-lb cylinder (residential / commercial): Holds about 23.6 gallons. Common for home heating systems, commercial cooking equipment, and temporary job-site heating. Typically sold and filled by dedicated propane dealers.
  • Bulk / permanent tanks (120-gallon and above): Installed on-property for residential heating or agricultural use. Rented or purchased through a full-service propane company with delivery.

Where to Buy Propane Tanks Near El Paso

If you need to purchase a new cylinder, here's where to find one in El Paso:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Stock new 20-lb empty cylinders (typically $35–$45 each) in the outdoor or grilling section. Also available as a pre-filled exchange tank.
  • Walmart: Carries new 20-lb cylinders in the sporting goods or outdoor department — usually $35–$50. Some locations also stock 1-lb camping cylinders.
  • Tractor Supply: Reliable source for larger cylinders including 33-lb and 40-lb tanks, plus 20-lb cylinders. Also offers propane refill service at many locations.
  • Local propane dealers: Dealers like Bob's Propane, B&B Propane, and DAE Propane in El Paso sell cylinders in all sizes and can fill them on the spot — often the best combination of price and convenience for larger tanks.
  • U-Haul locations: Several El Paso U-Haul centers sell new 20-lb tanks and also offer refill service. Good option if you're already renting a truck for a move or event.

Buy New vs Exchange vs Refill: Which Makes Sense?

Depending on your situation, one of these three options will be the right fit:

  • Buy new: Best if you don't own a cylinder yet, or if your existing tank is expired and can't be recertified. Costs $35–$50 for a 20-lb cylinder, then you need to fill it separately.
  • Exchange: Convenient when you're in a hurry — grab a pre-filled cylinder at Walmart, Home Depot, or most grocery stores without waiting. The catch: exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs (not the full 20-lb capacity), and cost $24–$28. That works out to $4.80–$5.50 per gallon.
  • Refill at a local dealer: Best value. A local propane dealer fills your existing cylinder to full capacity for $16–$21 — saving you $7–$12 compared to exchange. Over a grilling season, that adds up. The only tradeoff is a quick trip to the dealer during business hours.

Checking Your Tank Before You Go

Before buying a new tank or heading to a refill station, two quick checks can save you a wasted trip:

  • Expiration / recertification date: Look at the collar of the tank (the ring at the top around the valve). You'll see a stamped date like "06 14" — that means the tank was manufactured in June 2014. Propane cylinders have a 12-year service life; a tank made in June 2014 was due for recertification in June 2026. Many dealers will not fill an expired tank. If your tank is at or past its date, you can either have it recertified at a licensed dealer or replace it.
  • OPD valve: Tanks manufactured after 2002 should have an OPD (Overfill Protection Device) valve — you can identify it by the triangular handwheel. Older flat-handle valves are no longer refillable at most locations.

Propane Tanks for RVs and Camping Near El Paso

If you're heading out to the Guadalupe Mountains, White Sands, or camping along the Rio Grande, El Paso is a solid last stop for propane. Most dealers in the Northeast and Westside will fill 33-lb and 40-lb RV cylinders. For 1-lb camping cylinders, Walmart and sporting goods stores are the fastest option. For larger RV tanks (Manchester or DOT cylinders mounted on the tongue), call ahead to confirm the dealer has the right fitting before you bring your rig in.
