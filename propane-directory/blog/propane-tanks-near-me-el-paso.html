Searching for propane tanks near El Paso, TX? Where to Find Propane Tanks in El Paso

El Paso has three ways to get a propane tank, depending on whether you need to buy new, refill, or exchange:

Buy a New Propane Tank

  • Hardware and home improvement stores: Home Depot and Walmart locations across El Paso sell new 1-lb disposable canisters, standard 20-lb grill cylinders, and sometimes 40-lb tanks in the outdoor/seasonal section. Expect to pay $25–$40 for a new unfilled 20-lb cylinder.
  • Propane dealers: Local dealers like Ferrellgas often sell new cylinders alongside fill service — sometimes bundled with a first fill at a discount.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: TSC locations in the El Paso area carry a range of cylinder sizes, including 20-lb, 30-lb, and 40-lb tanks.

Refill Your Existing Tank

Refilling is the cheapest per-gallon option in El Paso. Dedicated propane dealers, U-Haul centers, and select Tractor Supply locations offer refill service — you bring your empty cylinder and they fill it by weight on the spot. See our El Paso propane refill guide for a full list of refill stations.

Exchange at a Cage

Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages sit outside most Walmart, Home Depot, and grocery store locations in El Paso. You hand in an empty tank and take a pre-filled one. It's fast, but exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs — not the full 20-lb capacity. See our El Paso propane exchange guide for cage locations and prices.

Tank Age and Recertification in El Paso

Before you take an old cylinder to a refill station in El Paso, check the date stamp on the collar. Propane cylinders must be recertified 12 years after manufacture and every 5 years after that. A tank stamped "06 14" was manufactured in June 2014 — it needed recertification in June 2026.

  • Most El Paso refill dealers will decline to fill an expired tank on the spot.
  • Some dealers offer recertification testing on-site — call ahead to ask.
  • If your tank is expired, it's often more cost-effective to exchange it at a cage (exchange programs accept expired tanks) or buy a new cylinder.
