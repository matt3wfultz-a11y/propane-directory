Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill & Exchange

November 27, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near El Paso? Whether you need a new cylinder, a refill on your existing tank, or a quick exchange at a retail cage, El Paso has options across the city — from the Lower Valley and East Side to the Westside and Northeast. This guide covers where to find propane tanks near you, what sizes are available, and what you'll pay in 2026.

Three Ways to Get a Propane Tank Near El Paso

When people search for "propane tanks near me," they usually want one of three things: a new empty tank to purchase, a filled tank to swap for their empty one, or a refill for their current cylinder. Here's where to go for each:

1. Buy a New Propane Tank

New propane cylinders are sold at hardware stores and home improvement retailers across El Paso. You can pick up a standard 20-lb grill tank at:

  • Home Depot — all El Paso locations carry 20-lb cylinders in the garden/outdoor section, usually unfilled
  • Lowe's — same selection as Home Depot; buy empty then fill or exchange at the cage outside
  • Ace Hardware / True Value — many local hardware stores carry 20-lb and 1-lb disposable cylinders
  • Tractor Supply — carries larger cylinders (20 lb, 40 lb, 100 lb) alongside propane refill service
  • Walmart Supercenter — 20-lb tanks available in the sporting goods section; exchange cages outside the entrance

For specialty sizes — 33-lb forklift tanks, 40-lb cylinders, or 100-lb residential tanks — your best source is a dedicated propane dealer in the El Paso area.

2. Exchange an Empty Tank

Propane exchange cages (Blue Rhino and AmeriGas) are the fastest way to get a filled tank when you're out. Bring an empty cylinder, pay at the register, and pick up a pre-filled replacement. Exchange locations in El Paso include virtually every Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, H-E-B, and most gas stations across the metro area.

Important: Exchange tanks are only filled to 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity. You're paying for speed and convenience, not a full tank.

3. Refill Your Existing Tank

Refilling at a local propane dealer gives you the best value — a full fill to your tank's rated capacity at the lowest per-gallon price. El Paso has dedicated refill stations from U-Haul locations and Tractor Supply to independent propane dealers serving residential and commercial customers. Refill vs Exchange: Which Is Right for You?

The right choice depends on your situation:

  • Choose exchange if you need a tank fast, don't want to wait at a fill station, or your current tank is too old to pass a refill inspection (look for the date stamped on the collar — tanks over 12 years old need recertification).
  • Choose refill if you want to save money, need a full fill, have a larger tank (33 lb, 40 lb, 100 lb), or use propane frequently enough that the savings add up.
