Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Fill & Exchange

September 18, 2026

When you search for propane tanks near El Paso, TX, you're usually trying to do one of three things: buy a new tank, get your existing tank refilled, or swap an empty at an exchange cage. El Paso has solid options for all three — spread across the Westside, Northeast, Lower Valley, and the border corridor. This guide covers every option, what you'll pay in 2026, and where to go based on what you actually need.

What Does "Propane Tanks Near Me" Usually Mean?

The search intent breaks down differently depending on your situation:

  • I need a refill — Your existing 20-lb or larger cylinder is empty and you want it filled by weight. This is almost always the cheapest option.
  • I need to exchange — You want to swap your empty tank for a pre-filled one at a retail cage (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas). Convenient, but costs more per gallon.
  • I need to buy a tank — You don't own a cylinder yet and need to pick one up before your first refill or exchange.
  • I need a large or specialty tank — You're looking for a 100-lb cylinder, a forklift tank, an RV tank, or a residential 500-gallon unit.

Each option has a different best location in El Paso. Read the section that matches your situation.

Option 1: Propane Tank Refill Near El Paso

Refilling your existing cylinder at a local dealer gives you the lowest cost per gallon and a fully filled tank — not the partially filled one you get with exchange programs. El Paso has several reliable refill sources:

  • Local propane dealers (Bob's Propane, B&B Propane, DAE Propane, West Texas Propane): Fill tanks of any size by weight. Best price, most knowledgeable staff. Call ahead for same-day service on larger cylinders.
  • U-Haul locations: Multiple El Paso U-Haul centers offer propane refill service for 20-lb and 33-lb cylinders. No appointment needed — just pull up to the fill station.
  • Tractor Supply (where available): Outdoor fill stations at select locations. Good option for grill cylinders in the northeast El Paso and Anthony corridor.

Option 2: Propane Tank Exchange Near El Paso

Exchange cages are the fastest option — swap your empty tank for a full one in under five minutes. In El Paso you'll find Blue Rhino and AmeriGas cages at:

  • Walmart Supercenter locations (Mesa, Zaragoza, Montwood, Alameda)
  • Home Depot on Montwood and Gateway West
  • Lowe's on Remcon Circle and Viscount
  • Kroger, Albertsons, and HEB stores throughout the city
  • Ace Hardware and True Value locations

Exchange is convenient but note that cage programs only fill tanks to 15 lbs, not the full 20-lb capacity. You're paying more for less propane. See the cost comparison below.

Option 3: Buy a New Propane Tank in El Paso

If you need a new cylinder, you have two paths:

  • Buy empty, then refill: Home Depot, Lowe's, and Walmart all sell empty 20-lb cylinders ($30–$50). Take it to a local dealer for your first fill.
  • Buy pre-filled via exchange: The exchange cages at Walmart and Home Depot let you pay a one-time "first tank" price that includes the deposit on a new cylinder. Convenient if you're starting from zero and need propane today.
  • Buy direct from a propane dealer: Local dealers sell new cylinders in all sizes — 20 lb, 33 lb, 40 lb, and 100 lb — often at better prices than big-box stores, and they'll fill it on the spot.

What to Check Before Getting Your Tank Filled

  • Check the date stamp: Look for a month/year stamped on the metal collar (e.g., "06 14" = June 2014). Tanks are valid for 12 years from manufacture. A tank stamped before September 2014 needs recertification before any El Paso dealer will refill it.
  • Check for damage: Rust, dents near the valve, or a bent collar are reasons a dealer may refuse to fill. Inspect before you drive over.
  • Valve type: All post-2002 20-lb cylinders have OPD (Overfill Protection Device) valves with a triangular handwheel. Older straight-handle valves won't be accepted for refill.
