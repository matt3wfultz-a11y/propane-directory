Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill & Exchange

September 4, 2026

Whether you need a brand-new propane cylinder, a refill on your existing tank, or a quick exchange at a local store, El Paso has options across the city. This guide covers where to find propane tanks near you in El Paso — including what you'll pay for each service type and which choice saves you the most money.

What "Propane Tanks Near Me" Usually Means

Most people searching for propane tanks near them want one of three things:

  • A refill — bring your empty cylinder to a dealer and pay by the gallon to fill it up
  • An exchange — drop off your old tank at a store cage and swap it for a pre-filled one
  • A new tank — buy a cylinder outright, either empty (to take to a refill station) or pre-filled

Each option has different costs and tradeoffs. Here's how each one works in El Paso.

Where to Refill a Propane Tank in El Paso

Refilling at a local propane dealer is almost always the cheapest option. El Paso has several dealers and service locations that fill tanks of any size by weight:

  • Bob's Propane (10289 Dyer St, Suite B) — a dedicated dealer serving Northeast El Paso with consistently high ratings
  • B&B Propane (5524 El Paso Dr) — serves the Lower Valley area
  • West Texas Propane — a regional supplier with bulk and cylinder fill capabilities
  • DAE Propane — serves residential and commercial customers across the metro area
  • U-Haul locations — several El Paso U-Haul centers offer outdoor refill service for 20-lb and 33-lb tanks
  • Tractor Supply — select locations offer outdoor propane refill at the fill station

See the full list with addresses and phone numbers in the El Paso propane directory.

Where to Exchange a Propane Tank in El Paso

Exchange cages (Blue Rhino and AmeriGas) are available at most major grocery stores, hardware stores, and big-box retailers in El Paso, including Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Albertsons, and H-E-B locations across the city. Exchange requires no appointment — just bring your empty tank, drop it in the cage, and walk out with a pre-filled one.

Where to Buy a New Propane Tank in El Paso

If you need a new cylinder, here are the best places to get one:

  • Hardware stores: Home Depot and Lowe's carry 20-lb cylinders, usually priced $30–$50 empty. Some locations also sell pre-filled tanks.
  • Walmart: Carries 1-lb disposable canisters and occasionally 20-lb cylinders. Pre-filled 20-lb tanks are sometimes available in the outdoor section.
  • Propane dealers: Local dealers like Bob's Propane and DAE Propane sell new cylinders at or near cost and can fill them on the spot.
  • Sporting goods stores: Academy Sports carries 1-lb camping canisters and some larger cylinders.

Things to Know Before You Go

  • Check the collar date: Tanks more than 12 years old require recertification before any dealer will refill them. Look for the stamped date on the collar ring (e.g., "09 14" = September 2014 — recertification due September 2026).
  • Transport safely: Carry propane tanks upright and secured, in a truck bed or with windows cracked if inside a vehicle cab.
  • No appointment needed: Most refill stations in El Paso are walk-up or drive-up — first come, first served. Exchange cages are available 24/7 at most retail locations.
  • Call ahead for large tanks: For 100-lb or larger cylinders, call the dealer first to confirm availability and current pricing.
