Three Ways to Get a Propane Tank Near You in El Paso

When El Paso residents search "propane tanks near me," they usually want one of three things:

  • Buy a new tank: Hardware stores, home improvement centers, and propane dealers sell empty cylinders ranging from portable 1-lb canisters to 100-lb stationary cylinders.
  • Refill an existing tank: Dedicated propane dealers and select Tractor Supply and U-Haul locations will fill your tank by weight — the most cost-effective option for anyone who already has a cylinder.
  • Exchange a tank: Exchange cages at Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, and most El Paso grocery stores let you swap an empty tank for a pre-filled 15-lb cylinder on the spot.

What to Know Before You Go

  • Check the collar date: Tanks must be recertified every 12 years. The manufacture date is stamped on the collar (e.g., "08 14" = August 2014). A tank due for recertification may be refused at a refill station.
  • Transport safely: Always carry propane cylinders upright, secured from tipping. In a car, crack the windows and go directly to your destination — never leave a filled tank in a hot vehicle.
  • Call ahead for large tanks: For 40-lb or 100-lb cylinders, some El Paso dealers prefer a call ahead to confirm availability.
  • Bring payment: Most El Paso refill stations accept cash and major credit cards.
