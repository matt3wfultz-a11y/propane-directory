Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Refill & Exchange

August 7, 2026

Whether you need a fresh propane tank for a cookout, a refill for your patio heater, or a larger cylinder for a generator, El Paso has options spread across the city. Where to Buy a New Propane Tank in El Paso

New 20 lb cylinders are widely available across El Paso at:

  • Hardware and home improvement stores: Home Depot, Lowe's, and Ace Hardware locations sell empty cylinders and pre-filled Blue Rhino or AmeriGas tanks. Empty cylinders run $35–$45; pre-filled exchange tanks are $50–$65.
  • Sporting goods stores: Academy Sports locations carry 1 lb camping cylinders, 20 lb grill tanks, and sometimes larger sizes.
  • Walmart: Most El Paso Walmarts sell 20 lb cylinders near the outdoor/seasonal section and also have exchange cages at the entrance.
  • Propane dealers: For anything larger than a 40 lb cylinder — including 100 lb, 250-gallon, or 500-gallon tanks — contact a local propane dealer directly. They sell, lease, and install large tanks.

If you already have a cylinder in good condition, skip the purchase and go straight to a refill — it's almost always cheaper than buying a new pre-filled tank.

Where to Refill a Propane Tank in El Paso

El Paso has multiple propane refill stations across its sprawling geography. The best options:

  • Dedicated propane dealers: Local dealers fill tanks of any size by weight, from 20 lb grill cylinders up to 100 lb and larger. They offer the lowest per-gallon price — typically $3.40–$4.50/gal in El Paso.
  • U-Haul locations: Several El Paso U-Haul centers provide propane refill service. They fill 20 lb and 33 lb cylinders by weight and are a reliable, no-appointment option.
  • Tractor Supply: The El Paso area Tractor Supply locations with outdoor fill stations can refill standard cylinders during store hours.
  • RV parks and dealers: Locations along I-10 serving RV travelers often have propane fill capability for larger cylinders and built-in motorhome tanks.

Where to Exchange a Propane Tank in El Paso

Exchange cages are available at most major retail locations in El Paso. You drop off your empty tank and walk away with a different pre-filled one. Common exchange cage locations include:

  • Walmart (multiple El Paso locations)
  • Home Depot and Lowe's
  • H-E-B grocery stores
  • Walgreens and CVS (select locations)
  • Circle K and Stripes convenience stores

Most cages are stocked with Blue Rhino or AmeriGas tanks. Some locations let you do an online exchange and pick up in-store. But read the price section below before you commit — exchange is convenient, not cheap.

Checking Your Tank Before You Go

Before heading to any El Paso propane location, inspect your cylinder. Dealers and refill stations in Texas must refuse to fill tanks that fail certain checks:

  • Date stamp: Look at the collar. A stamp like "06 14" means June 2014. Standard cylinders expire after 12 years — that tank would have been due for recertification in June 2026. See our guide to propane tank recertification if yours is near expiration.
  • Rust and damage: Surface rust on the outside is cosmetic. Deep pitting, dents near the valve, or a damaged OPD valve (the triangle-handle safety valve) may disqualify the tank.
  • OPD valve: All cylinders 4–40 lbs sold since 2002 must have an OPD (Overfill Protection Device). Older tanks without one cannot be legally refilled.
