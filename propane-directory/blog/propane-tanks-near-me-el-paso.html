Propane Tanks Near Me in El Paso, TX: Buy, Rent & Exchange

July 31, 2026

Searching for propane tanks near you in El Paso? Whether you need a new 20-lb cylinder for the grill, a 100-lb tank for a patio heater, or a large residential tank for your home, El Paso has multiple options across the city. This guide covers where to buy, rent, or exchange propane tanks in El Paso — and what you'll pay for each.

Types of Propane Tanks Available in El Paso

Before heading out, it helps to know which tank type you need. El Paso suppliers carry the full range:

  • 20-lb cylinder (standard grill tank): The most common size. Holds about 4.7 gallons. Available to buy empty or pre-filled at most hardware stores and grocery outlets across El Paso.
  • 33-lb cylinder (forklift / large RV): Used in forklifts and some large RV setups. Available at propane dealers and U-Haul locations.
  • 40-lb cylinder: A step up from the grill tank — popular for patio heaters and food trucks. Available at propane dealers citywide.
  • 100-lb cylinder: Common for supplemental heating, generators, and commercial uses. Typically purchased from a propane dealer; some hardware stores stock these.
  • 250–500 gallon tank (residential): For whole-home or large-appliance propane. Dealers lease these tanks and deliver propane on a schedule or on-call basis.

Where to Get a Propane Tank in El Paso

Buy an Empty or Pre-Filled Tank

For 20-lb and 40-lb cylinders, the easiest places to buy in El Paso are:

  • Home Depot and Lowe's: Stock new empty 20-lb cylinders (typically $25–$40) and operate Blue Rhino and AmeriGas exchange cages for pre-filled swaps.
  • Walmart: Pre-filled 20-lb exchange tanks available at most El Paso locations via Blue Rhino or AmeriGas kiosks.
  • Tractor Supply Co.: Carries new empty cylinders in multiple sizes plus operates a propane refill station — a good one-stop option for the El Paso area.
  • Sporting goods stores: Academy Sports and similar retailers carry 1-lb disposable canisters and 20-lb cylinders for camping and grilling use.
  • Propane dealers: Ferrellgas, AmeriGas dealer locations, and independent El Paso propane suppliers sell 20-lb through 100-lb cylinders directly. Prices are often lower than retail chains.

Rent or Lease a Large Propane Tank

If you need a 100-gallon or larger tank for your home or business in El Paso, the standard model is a dealer lease:

  • The dealer owns the tank and places it on your property
  • You pay for propane deliveries at the dealer's per-gallon rate
  • Some dealers charge an annual tank rental fee ($50–$100/year); others waive it for customers with regular usage
  • Moving? The dealer retrieves their tank — you're not responsible for transporting or storing it

Contact local El Paso dealers directly to compare lease terms. Ferrellgas and Suburban Propane both operate in the El Paso metro; independent local dealers may offer more competitive per-gallon rates for residential customers.

Exchange an Empty Tank

Exchange cages swap your empty 20-lb cylinder for a pre-filled one. El Paso exchange locations include Blue Rhino and AmeriGas kiosks at:

  • Walmart (multiple El Paso locations)
  • Home Depot and Lowe's
  • Most H-E-B and major grocery stores across the city
  • Walgreens and CVS locations (1-lb disposable canisters only)

Exchange is fast and convenient but costs more per gallon than a refill. See the cost comparison below before deciding.

Tank Certification: What to Check Before You Buy or Refill

All propane cylinders have a manufacture date stamped on the collar — the ring around the valve at the top. A standard 20-lb cylinder must be recertified or retired 12 years after manufacture, and again every 5 years after recertification. If you're buying a used tank or pulling an old one out of storage, check the collar stamp before taking it in for a refill. A dealer will refuse to fill an expired cylinder.

  • Collar stamp format: "MM YY" — e.g., "06 14" means June 2014
  • A tank stamped 06 14 expired in June 2026 — it needs recertification now
  • Recertification typically costs $25–$40 at a propane dealer; some dealers do it on-site
  • Tanks more than 12 years old that haven't been recertified cannot be legally refilled
