About Propane Services in Arlington

Arlington sits squarely between Dallas and Fort Worth in the heart of the DFW Metroplex, making it one of the most accessible cities in North Texas for propane services. Home to AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field, Arlington is also a densely populated suburban city where propane is widely used for outdoor grilling, patio heaters, and residential backup systems. Exchange kiosks are plentiful at grocery and hardware stores throughout the city.

Arlington's central location in Tarrant County means residents are also within easy driving distance of suppliers in nearby Grand Prairie, Mansfield, and Irving. The listings above focus on businesses located within or immediately adjacent to Arlington proper.

Frequently Asked Questions — Propane in Arlington

Where can I refill my propane tank in Arlington, TX?

Arlington has propane refill and exchange locations at hardware stores, grocery stores, and dedicated propane suppliers across the city. Use the listings above to find the nearest location with addresses, phone numbers, and customer ratings.

What is the difference between propane refill and exchange in Arlington?

A propane refill tops off your existing cylinder to its full capacity — you pay only for the gas used. A propane exchange swaps your empty cylinder for a pre-filled one, but exchange tanks typically contain only about 15 lbs rather than the full 20-lb capacity. Refill is typically the better value when a refill station is available nearby.

How much does propane cost in Arlington?

Propane refill prices in Arlington typically range from $3.00 to $4.50 per gallon. Prices fluctuate with market conditions and are generally higher in winter. Call ahead to confirm current pricing at your preferred supplier.

Are there propane suppliers near AT&T Stadium in Arlington?

Yes, the Arlington Entertainment District area has several propane exchange locations at nearby grocery and hardware stores. For the most accurate and current locations, use the map and listings above or call suppliers directly for directions.
