About Propane Services in Texas

Texas is the largest propane-consuming state in the country. With vast rural areas, a booming agricultural sector, and millions of homes using propane for grills, heaters, and whole-home systems, the Lone Star State has one of the most extensive networks of propane dealers and refill stations anywhere in the nation.

From the Gulf Coast refineries near Houston and Corpus Christi to the high plains of West Texas, propane supply infrastructure runs deep. Major metros like Dallas, San Antonio, Austin, and Fort Worth have dozens of refill stations and exchange kiosks. Smaller towns and rural counties — including communities along the Rio Grande like Fabens, Socorro, Canutillo, and Anthony — are served by regional propane dealers who handle both retail fills and home delivery.

Texas summers push outdoor grillers to burn through propane at a high clip, and the occasional hard freeze reminds homeowners why having a reliable propane backup matters. Whether you need a 20-lb grill cylinder refilled or a 500-gallon residential tank topped off before winter, this directory covers suppliers across the state.

Refill vs. Exchange in Texas

Most Texas hardware stores and grocery chains carry propane exchange cages (Blue Rhino, AmeriGas). These are convenient but typically only fill tanks to about 15 lbs — not the full 20-lb capacity. Dedicated refill stations fill your tank to its rated capacity and charge per gallon, which usually works out 30–40% cheaper per pound of propane. If a refill station is within a few miles, it's almost always worth the extra stop.

Propane Prices in Texas

Retail propane prices in Texas typically range from $3.00 to $4.50 per gallon depending on location, season, and supplier. Prices are generally lower near the Gulf Coast refineries and higher in remote West Texas towns. Winter months bring higher demand, especially in North Texas where cold snaps drive up heating usage. Call ahead to get the current rate at any listed location.

Large Tank Fills and Delivery

Many Texas suppliers handle tanks larger than the standard 20-lb grill cylinder. Suppliers marked "Refill" on city pages often accommodate 100-lb cylinders on-site. For residential 250- or 500-gallon tanks, most areas are served by regional delivery companies — contact the supplier directly to confirm delivery range and minimum order requirements.
